# 3DLOOK Terminology & Language Guardrails

> Источник: Google Doc от Ассель (обновляется периодически).  
> Применяется ко ВСЕМУ корпоративному контенту: SEO-статьи, лендинги, соцсети.

## General Approaches

### Abbreviations
- Перша згадка: повна форма + абревіатура в дужках (напр. «Body Mass Index (BMI)»)
- Виняток: загальновідомі (AI, WWW, iOS)

### Internal & Third-Party Linking
- Інтегрувати посилання прямо в anchor text (не голі URL, не «click here»)

## Terminology Rules

### ❌ Objective
- **When:** In relation to 3DLOOK's technology and product outputs
- **To apply:** Rarely
- **To avoid:** Most cases
- **Reason:** Too strong and subjective
- **Replace with:** standardized, timestamped, structured, repeatable

### ⚠️ We / our
- **When:** Corporate content
- **To apply:** When ownership matters — company is making a clear claim, explaining its own product, or taking responsibility
- **To avoid:** When the workflow or buyer's needs should be the focus
- **Reason:** If overused, content feels like a company-centered sales deck
- **Fix:** Reformulate the statement

### ⚠️ You
- **When:** Corporate content
- **To apply:** Landing pages, conversion sections, product onboarding, practical guidance — when speaking to a clear decision-maker
- **To avoid:** Neutral educational sections — article should feel authoritative
- **Reason:** Creates direct address; inappropriate for educational tone
- **Fix:** Reformulate the statement

### ❌ Reader / audience / the following sections / below / etc.
- **When:** Corporate content
- **To apply:** NEVER
- **To avoid:** Almost always
- **Reason:** Reminds people they're reading marketing content instead of focusing on the actual problem
- **Fix:** Don't describe the reading experience. Describe the business reality.

### ❌ This article / this guide / our article / our content
- **When:** Corporate content
- **To apply:** In scope notes, define the document
- **To avoid:** Preferably — consumes the strongest part of the page without adding meaning
- **Fix:** Reformulate the statement

### ❌ By hand
- **When:** Corporate content
- **To apply:** NEVER
- **To avoid:** Always
- **Reason:** Too plain
- **Replace with:** Manually

## Application in SEO Articles

При написанні SEO-статей через пайплайн (planner → writer → editor → publisher):

1. **Writer:** не використовувати objective, reader/audience, this article/guide, by hand. Обережно з we/our і you.
2. **Editor:** Pass 3b повинен включати перевірку термінології — grep по забороненим словам.
3. **Publisher:** checklist повинен включати terminology check.
