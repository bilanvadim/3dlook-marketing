# Product imagery — from the live site

Collected 2026-11-12 from the most recently published pages of 3dlook.ai (`/pricing/` live 2026-11-05,
`/for-insurance-underwriting/` live 2026-10-21, `/wrist-measurement/`, the telehealth structured-data page
of 2026-07 and the `/technology/` hero of 2026-06). Older 2024-25 homepage imagery is deliberately left out.
These are the three carrier assets from `DESIGN.md` §10 (3D render, guided-capture phone UI, dashboard in a
laptop frame) plus the Wrist and Mobile Tailor sets.

**Rules**
- Product UI in these images is real; do not generate fake product UI with image models when one of these fits.
- Numbers visible inside a UI (weight, fat %, BMI) are illustrative screen content, not claims. Never quote them in copy.
- Client and press logos from the same pages are **not** here: client names follow `product-info/` and outbound rules
  (no client names in outbound, Generation Tux not in new materials, no MT client logos on FX pages).
- Product colour coding (`DESIGN.md` §2): FitXpress blue, Wrist violet, Mobile Tailor monochrome. Keep images in
  their product's context.
- Refresh: when a newer page ships, re-collect from it and replace, so this folder always mirrors the latest site.
