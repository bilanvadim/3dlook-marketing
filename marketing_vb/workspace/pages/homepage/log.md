## 2026-11-13 · Hero v3.1: luxury mannequin head, one metric at a time, product colours

Vadim on v3: «голова сильно на яйце схожа. давай візьмемо щось з манекенів для люксових брендів», «щоб всі показники цифри не світились а показувались по черзі», «просто давай фінгер. без рінг», then «фон давай змінювати по кольорах коли ми підсвічуємо що. хелс - синій. фінгер, вріст - фіолетовий, заміри тіла - мобайл тейлор - світлий білий люксовий».

- **Head.** New `tools/hero_head.py` (numpy): a couture window-mannequin head instead of the egg. The source MakeHuman head is voxelized, morphologically closed (the parted lips and eyelid folds close; a plug seals the neck) and turned into a blurred distance field: a soft sculpted face with nose, brow, cheekbones and chin, no ears, no eye or lip detail. On top: slicked-back sculpted hair (a shell that grows from nothing at the forehead hairline and fades out over the ears) and a low chignon; the head is raised on a slimmer neck, chin up 5°. Meshed as a ring lattice from the body's neck loop, ray-cast, relaxed tangentially on the surface; a Taubin band hides the seam. Mesh: 28.9k vertices (19.8k on the head), dots on the head at the body's spacing.
- **One metric at a time.** No columns of cards any more. When the lens reaches a point on the body, that one card opens beside it with the eyebrow of its product group, the leader line draws itself out from the point and the value counts up; the previous card fades first. Deterministic state machine stepped at 60 Hz (`renderAt(t)` replays one loop on a jump, so frame capture and the loop seam stay exact).
- **Product colours.** The scene takes the colour of the open card: FitXpress blue (BMI, lean mass, body fat), Wrist violet (`--wm-deep` #1B0942 family; wrist, finger), Mobile Tailor paper and ink (bust, waist, hips, thigh: paper backdrop, white cards with ink text, the x-ray under the lens drawn in ink on frosted paper, ink tape loops). The new colour spreads from the lens in a soft circle (1.1 s) instead of a crossfade, which went through a muddy grey between navy and paper.
- **Finger card** says «Finger» (was «Ring finger»); the eyebrow stays «Wrist and fingers». The fingers claims flag from v3 still applies.
- **Loop** 20 s (was 16): BMI → bust → waist → hips → thigh → finger → wrist → body fat → lean mass; ~2.2 s per metric. Frame 0 (and the poster) shows the BMI card on blue.
- **Prototype.** `page-v3-2026-11-13.html`: the video now sits in a rounded panel; the edge fade made the paper phase glow against the navy hero.
