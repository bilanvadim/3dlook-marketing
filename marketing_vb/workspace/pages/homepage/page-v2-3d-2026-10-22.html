How it works

How 3D body scanning fits into your product

Mobile 3D body scanning estimates body shape and body measurements from photos taken on an ordinary smartphone camera, with no booth-style 3D body scanner or wearable. Each 3DLOOK 3D body scan needs two photos: one from the front and one from the side.

  1. 01
    CaptureGuided photos in your app or website, with the 3DLOOK software development kit (SDK)Customer side
  2. 02
    ProcessThe 3DLOOK application programming interface (API) turns the photos into measurements, estimates and a 3D model3DLOOK side
  3. 03
    Use the resultsYour backend receives structured results, and your product shows them where the workflow needs themCustomer side

Frequently asked questions

What does 3DLOOK do?

3DLOOK builds mobile measurement technology for businesses. Its body scanning turns two smartphone photos into body measurements, body composition estimates and 3D body models, delivered through an API and SDKs. FitXpress serves health, fitness and insurance programs, Mobile Tailor serves apparel and uniform companies, and AI Wrist Measurement serves jewelry, watch and wearable brands.

Which 3DLOOK product fits my business?

Health programs, insurers, online pharmacies and fitness apps use FitXpress for body measurements, BMI and body composition estimates. Made-to-measure brands, manufacturers and uniform programs use Mobile Tailor for garment measurements. Jewelry, watch and wearable brands use AI Wrist Measurement for wrist sizing.

Do all 3DLOOK products use the same scan?

FitXpress and Mobile Tailor use the same body scan: two guided photos, one from the front and one from the side. AI Wrist Measurement has its own flow: two guided photos of the wrist with a standard-size card as a reference, and the result in millimeters is delivered through an API and an SDK.

Can the scan run inside our own app?

Yes. The 3DLOOK SDKs place the guided capture inside the business's own app or web flow, and the results return through the API. Onboarding, consent wording, loading screens and the results screen follow the business's own design. The photo capture layer stays fixed, because its pose and phone-angle checks protect measurement accuracy.

How do I get access and pricing?

FitXpress and Mobile Tailor plans are listed on the pricing page, and Mobile Tailor starts with a 7-day trial. Access to the API and SDKs starts with a demo call, where the team confirms the workflow and the integration route.
