About 3DLOOK

Mobile measurement.
Built since 2016.

Meet 3DLOOK

3DLOOK develops mobile measurement technology for businesses, helping teams collect body and wrist data through guided smartphone capture.

Winner of the 2019 Retail Digital Transformation Grand Challenge, run by the 3D Retail Coalition with Kalypso and IEEE. Participant in the IEEE 3D Body Processing working group.

Common questions

Find the right way
to get started.

Choose by measurement need and workflow. Each product has its own capture, outputs and access options.

Which product fits my business?

FitXpress provides body data for health, fitness and insurance workflows. Mobile Tailor provides body measurements for apparel, manufacturing and uniform programs. 3DLOOK AI Wrist Measurement provides wrist circumference data for jewelry, watches and wrist-worn devices.

Do all products use the same scan?

Body measurement uses guided front and side photos. Wrist sizing uses a dedicated flow with two wrist photos. Each flow returns outputs designed for its measurement task.

Do we need to build an integration?

Guided capture and results can connect to your app or website through supported API and SDK options. For apparel measurements, scan links and a dashboard provide a route without a backend integration.

Where can we see pricing or start a trial?

Compare available plans on the pricing page. A trial route is available for apparel measurement. For integrated deployments and wrist-sizing pricing, talk to the team.
