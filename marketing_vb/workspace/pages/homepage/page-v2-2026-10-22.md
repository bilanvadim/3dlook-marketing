## How 3D body scanning fits into your product

Mobile 3D body scanning estimates body shape and body measurements from photos taken on an ordinary smartphone camera, with no booth-style 3D body scanner or wearable. Each 3DLOOK 3D body scan needs two photos: one from the front and one from the side.

<!-- flow strip; environment label under each node -->
1. **Your app or website** · guided capture with the 3DLOOK software development kit (SDK) · *customer side*
2. **Your backend** · sends the photos and orchestrates application programming interface (API) calls · *customer side*
3. **3DLOOK API** · processes the photos into measurements, estimates and a 3D model · *3DLOOK side*
4. **Your backend** · receives the structured results · *customer side*
5. **Your product** · shows the results where your workflow needs them · *customer side*

**3DLOOK provides**
- Guided capture with pose, framing and clothing checks
- Photo processing and the body model
- A structured results payload
- Integration documentation and support

**Your team builds**
- The user journey and onboarding
- Consent and privacy messaging
- Where the results appear in your product
- Your own decision rules and workflows

Mobile Tailor also works without any integration: a business sends a scan link or adds a measurement widget to its site, and the results appear in the Mobile Tailor dashboard.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does 3DLOOK do?
3DLOOK builds mobile body-scanning technology that turns two smartphone photos into body measurements, body composition estimates and 3D body models for businesses, delivered through an API and SDKs. FitXpress serves health and fitness programs, Mobile Tailor serves apparel and uniform companies, and Wrist Measurement serves jewelry, watch and wearable brands.

### Which 3DLOOK product fits my business?
Health programs, insurers, online pharmacies and fitness apps use FitXpress for body measurements, BMI and body composition estimates. Made-to-measure brands, manufacturers and uniform programs use Mobile Tailor for garment measurements. Jewelry, watch and wearable brands use Wrist Measurement for wrist sizing.

### Can the scan run inside our own app?
Yes. The web and mobile SDKs place the guided capture inside the business's own app or web flow, and the results return through the API. Onboarding, consent wording, loading screens and the results screen follow the business's own design. The photo capture layer stays fixed, because its pose and phone-angle checks protect measurement accuracy.

### How do I get access and pricing?
FitXpress and Mobile Tailor plans are listed on the [pricing page](/pricing/). Access to the API and SDKs starts with a demo call, where the team confirms the workflow and the integration route.
