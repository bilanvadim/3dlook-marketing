What this prototype shows

Five top-level items for any number of products: Products, Industries, Pricing, Resources, Company. Log in and Book a demo sit on the right. Switch the product count above: the header itself never changes, only the inside of the Products and Industries panels.

  • Up to 6 products: one card per product in a grid.
  • From 7 products: columns by product family (Body · Hand and wrist · Head).
  • Industries: one column per industry family; only published pages; 6 links per column, then "All … use cases".
  • Every choice appears once. Technology is the first row of Products; Accuracy and Privacy sit in its footer row.
