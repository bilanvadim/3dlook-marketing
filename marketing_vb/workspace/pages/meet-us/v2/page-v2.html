How do you find booth 55630?

Booth 55630 is a corner booth in the Health area of Halls A-D, on Level 2 of the Venetian Expo. Pick where you come from and follow the route.

  1. 1Leave the escalators and walk along the Venetian Ballroom, keeping it on your right.
  2. 2At the end of the ballroom, turn right into the corridor to the Hall D entrance.
  3. 3Go through the Hall D entrance and straight on to the main Health aisle.
  4. 4Turn right and walk about 40 m, then turn left into a narrow aisle.
  5. 5Booth 55630 is about 25 m ahead on your left, at the corner.
  1. 1Walk along the west concourse to the Hall C entrance.
  2. 2Go through the Hall C entrance and follow the aisle to the main Health aisle.
