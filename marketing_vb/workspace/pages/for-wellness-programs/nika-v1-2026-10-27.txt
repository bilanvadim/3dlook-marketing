The problem

## Scale weight often stays flat while the member's body changes

Scale weight can hold steady for weeks while waist, hip and body-fat change. When weight is the only physical signal, the change a member makes stays outside the progress view.

- 

### The scale hides change
A member who loses fat and holds lean mass sees a flat number. The app has nothing else to show at the check-in.

- 

### Progress photos do not compare
Camera-roll photos differ in angle, distance and lighting. Neither the member nor the coach can read a change from them with confidence.

- 

### Personalization stops at the survey
Content and coaching prompts run on a stated goal and a self-reported weight. Members on the same goal receive the same journey.

- Employee wellness

### Program reports rest on self-report
Employer clients ask what changed in the program. Self-reported weigh-ins give the platform little to report.

Progress view

## Show members where their body changed between check-ins

Each follow-up scan is compared with the baseline. The progress view shows the measurements that moved, the direction of change and the date of each scan.

Body-fat percentage is shown as a dated trend with a plain-language explanation, which reduces the risk of overinterpreting a single value.

Outputs

## The same record format at baseline and at every check-in

FitXpress returns each scan as a timestamped record through the application programming interface (API). The fields stay the same from scan to scan, and each record is ready to compare with the one before it.

 Body measurements

### 80+ body measurements
Waist, hip, chest, thigh and other dimensions in centimeters, produced by the same processing at every scan.
 Body composition

### Body composition estimates
Body-fat percentage, fat mass and lean mass estimates. Body mass index (BMI) and basal metabolic rate (BMR) are calculated metrics.
 3D model

### 3D body model and 3D Body Progress
A 3D model reconstructed from each scan. On Pro, compare two scans in a 3D view and show a goal with 3D Goal Visualization.
 Capture quality

### Pose and clothing checks
Real-Time Pose Validation and the Clothing Detector correct the capture during the shot. Their results return with each scan for your retake rules.
 Comparison

### Timestamp and scan identifier
Each record shows when it was captured. Your platform selects two scan identifiers, and FitXpress returns the comparison between them.
 Data delivery

### Formats for your app and analytics
JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) through the API, comma-separated values (CSV) export from the FitXpress Admin Panel and 3D model export.

FitXpress is not a medical device. It provides structured body data for wellness and coaching experiences; how results are presented and used stays with your platform.

How it works

## Add a two-photo scan to your app's check-in flow

Your team embeds FitXpress through the API and web and mobile software development kits (SDKs). The capture flow is white-label, and members stay inside your app throughout.

- 

### Baseline scan with your consent screen
Your app explains which data is captured, how it is used and how long it is kept. The member then completes the first guided front and side capture.

- 

### Check-ins under the same conditions
The member repeats the scan with the same pose and similar conditions. For body-change programs, 4 to 12 weeks between scans is a practical starting range.

- 

### Comparison into the experience
Results return through webhook events, with polling as the documented fallback. Your app updates the progress view and triggers the next step.

Native SDKs capture on the device and work offline; processing starts when the photos reach the API. 3DLOOK’s mobile body scanning technology explains the capture and processing approach.

Validation and data handling

## Check repeatability and data handling before the first check-in ships

A progress view compares each member with themselves. Repeatability decides whether a change between two scans is real or noise. Accuracy against expert manual measurement shows how a single value relates to a tape measure.

< 1 cmTypical scan-to-scan difference for most evaluated body measurements.

96 to 97%Body-measurement accuracy against expert manual measurement, with typical absolute error of 1.5 to 2.0 cm.

0.89 cmWaist repeatability across repeated scans.

2.14 cmWaist mean absolute error against expert manual measurement, in the same study.

These figures come from internal validation and have not been peer-reviewed. The validation population covered ages 16 to 78, weights of 38 to 210 kg and heights of 150 to 220 cm, from the US and Europe. Accuracy is not published by body shape or skin tone. Results depend on form-fitting clothing, a completed guided capture and an entered height within 2 cm. Body composition values are estimates. Supporting methodology is available under a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

Body scanning accuracy, repeatability and validation methodology

- Photo retentionFitXpress deletes photos immediately after processing or retains them for up to 30 days under a contract-specific policy. Retained photos are blurred, and face obfuscation is applied during capture.

- Member identifiersFitXpress does not receive names or contact details. Scan records use randomly generated identifiers, which your platform maps to member profiles.

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)In most deployments, your platform acts as data controller and 3DLOOK acts as data processor. A data processing agreement (DPA) with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) covers international transfers, with a UK Addendum where UK GDPR applies.

- HostingFitXpress data is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), primarily in the US-West-2 region and partly in the US-East-1 region.

- App release requirementsYour app's privacy policy, consent screen and health and fitness entries in App Store privacy details and the Google Play Data safety form cover the scan. Your team owns that wording.

- Encryption and model trainingData is encrypted in transit with Transport Layer Security (TLS) and at rest. 3DLOOK does not use production customer data to train its models without the customer’s explicit, documented authorization.

- DeletionYour platform requests deletion of specific scan outputs by sending their scan identifiers to 3DLOOK.

- Regulatory statusAn independent regulatory assessment concluded that FitXpress does not meet the medical-device definition under the UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (UK MDR) or the EU Medical Devices Regulation (EU MDR) for its current intended purpose. FitXpress is not cleared, authorized or approved by the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA).

The FitXpress data, privacy, security and regulatory frequently asked questions cover hosting, deletion, SOC 2 and FDA information. Privacy contact: privacy@3dlook.me.

Frequently asked questions

## Questions from wellness product, coaching and data teams
How often should members scan?
For body-change programs, 4 to 12 weeks between scans is a practical starting range, adjusted to the program goal and the expected rate of change. Consistent capture conditions matter more than frequency, because repeatability decides whether the change between two scans is real.
Which results should members see?
Your platform decides. A focused progress view shows the measurements tied to the member's goal, for example the waist for a waist goal or fat mass and lean mass estimates for a body composition goal. Labels state which outputs are measurements and which are estimates.
Can members use their own phones?
Yes. Capture runs on iOS 15 or later and on Android phones, including older models, which may show a lower frame rate during capture. No other equipment is needed. The member stands for the scan, and the current dataset does not support wheelchair users or amputees. Two gender options are available. Height, weight and gender are required inputs, and an entered height more than 2 cm off affects results.
Does FitXpress measure muscle?
FitXpress reports fat mass and lean mass estimates. Lean mass includes organs, bone and water along with muscle; it is not a direct measurement of skeletal muscle.
Does 3DLOOK know which scans belong to which member?
No. Scan records carry randomly generated identifiers. Your platform links them to member profiles and selects the two scans to compare, and FitXpress returns the comparison between those records.
Can we use scan results for wellness rewards or incentives?
Programs that connect body data to rewards need additional governance, a privacy review and clear program rules. Reward and eligibility determinations stay with the program administrator. FitXpress for health plans and employer wellness covers remote verification for incentive programs.
Who handles member consent and privacy notices?
In most deployments, your platform is the data controller. Your team provides the privacy notice, sets the lawful basis, obtains consent where the workflow requires it and defines retention. 3DLOOK processes scan data on your behalf under the DPA. Where the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) applies to your deployment, 3DLOOK can act as a business associate under an executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
How does scan data reach our app?
Results return through the API as JSON, delivered through webhook events with polling as the documented fallback. Webhooks currently have no automatic retries or signature verification, and polling is the recommended backup. The FitXpress Admin Panel provides a central view of scan results and CSV export.
Is FitXpress a medical device?
No. An independent regulatory assessment concluded that FitXpress does not meet the medical-device definition under UK MDR or EU MDR for its current intended purpose. FitXpress is not cleared, authorized or approved by the FDA. It does not diagnose conditions or make health decisions.
