The problem

Why does the body-measurement step still wait for a screening day?

Biometric screening is an in-person check by a medical professional: BMI, blood pressure, cholesterol and glucose. Half of large employers with a screening program offer no remote way to complete it, so their hybrid and remote employees have to come in for the measurement.

62%

of US firms with 200 or more workers and a biometric screening program use incentives or penalties to get employees to complete it.

KFF Employer Health Benefits Survey.
78%

of US employees with remote-capable jobs work hybrid (52%) or fully remote (26%).

Gallup Hybrid Work indicator.

FitXpress moves the body-measurement part of the screening to the employee’s phone: waist and 80+ body measurements, BMI from entered height and weight, and body composition estimates. Blood pressure and blood tests stay with the screening provider.

Screening scope

What FitXpress adds to a biometric screening program

FitXpress covers body dimensions and related calculated or estimated metrics. It can complement onsite biometric screening and other services for the components that require clinical equipment or laboratory testing.

Measured body dimensions

Waist and 80+ body measurements

Waist, hip, chest, thigh and other circumferences and dimensions generated from guided front and side smartphone photos.

Employees complete this step at home, with no screening appointment.

Calculated body metric

BMI from height and weight

BMI is calculated from height and weight inputs. It is not directly measured from the photos. Weight-source verification is available through Smart Scales.

BMI comes back in the same record, so there is nothing extra for administrators to collect.

Estimated body metrics

Body composition estimates

Body-fat percentage, fat mass and lean mass are estimates. They should be identified as estimates in employee-facing results and any program reporting.

Each scan adds a progress measure beyond weight.

Provided separately

Blood pressure and blood tests

FitXpress does not measure blood pressure, blood glucose, cholesterol or laboratory biomarkers. Programs that require them must use appropriate separate screening methods.

Onsite or lab visits are needed only for blood pressure and blood tests.

FitXpress is not a medical device.

Program integrity

A capture flow built to make submissions easier to check

When rewards depend on a result, the record has to hold up in a dispute or an audit.

  • Camera-only capture

    Employees take photos in the guided flow; uploading pictures from a phone gallery is not supported.

  • Checks during capture

    Real-Time Pose Validation guides pose and framing. The Clothing Detector identifies clothing conditions that may interfere with capture and can prompt corrective action.

  • A dated record

    Each scan returns the same structured fields with a timestamp and scan identifier. Your program links scans to the participant and compares checkpoints.

These controls support fraud review. They do not replace program rules or administrator review.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about remote biometric screening

Can FitXpress replace onsite biometric screening?

FitXpress provides the body-measurement component of remote biometric screening. Participants can complete guided scans at home for waist and other body dimensions, BMI and body composition estimates. Blood pressure measurements and blood tests require separate screening methods.

How can corporate wellness platforms and employee wellness apps integrate FitXpress?

FitXpress offers API and software development kit (SDK) integration with guided smartphone capture. The wellness platform links scan results to its participant records, screening workflow and reporting tools.

Can employees complete biometric body measurements on their own phones?

Yes. Employees follow visual and voice instructions to capture front and side photos using a supported smartphone. Participants who cannot use the current standing capture flow need an alternative screening method.

What privacy and security controls are available for employee wellness apps?

FitXpress supports guided live capture, scan identifiers and data processing arrangements. Where HIPAA applies to a specific deployment, 3DLOOK can act as a business associate under an executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA). In most enterprise deployments, the customer acts as the data controller and 3DLOOK acts as the data processor under GDPR.

How accurate are FitXpress body measurements?

Internal validation reports 96-97% body-measurement accuracy against expert manual measurement, with typical absolute error of 1.5-2.0 cm. Typical scan-to-scan differences are under 1 cm for most evaluated measurements. Results depend on capture conditions, clothing and correct height input. Body composition outputs are estimates.
