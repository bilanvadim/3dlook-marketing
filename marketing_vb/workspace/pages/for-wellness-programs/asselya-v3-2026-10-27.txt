Screening scope

## What FitXpress adds to a biometric screening program
FitXpress covers body dimensions and related calculated or estimated metrics. It can complement onsite biometric screening and other services for the components that require clinical equipment or laboratory testing.

Measured body dimensions

### Waist and 80+ body measurements
Waist, hip, chest, thigh and other circumferences and dimensions generated from guided front and side smartphone photos.
Calculated body metric

### BMI from height and weight
BMI is calculated from height and weight inputs. It is not directly measured from the photos. Weight-source verification through Smart Scales is in beta and should be confirmed for the intended deployment.
Estimated body metrics

### Body composition estimates
Body-fat percentage, fat mass and lean mass are estimates. They should be identified as estimates in employee-facing results and any program reporting.
Provided separately

### Blood pressure and blood tests
FitXpress does not measure blood pressure, blood glucose, cholesterol or laboratory biomarkers. Programs that require them must use appropriate separate screening methods.

FitXpress is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions, interpret screening results, or determine employee eligibility or incentives.

Frequently asked questions

## Questions about remote biometric screening
Can FitXpress replace onsite biometric screening?
FitXpress provides the body-measurement component of remote biometric screening. Participants can complete guided scans at home for waist and other body dimensions, BMI and body composition estimates. Blood pressure measurements and blood tests require separate screening methods.
What data does FitXpress collect for an employee wellness program?
FitXpress returns 80+ body measurements, including waist and hip dimensions, alongside BMI calculated from height and weight, body composition estimates, capture-quality information and scan identifiers. Results are delivered through an application programming interface (API).
How can corporate wellness platforms and employee wellness apps integrate FitXpress?
FitXpress offers API and software development kit (SDK) integration with guided smartphone capture. The wellness platform links scan results to its participant records, screening workflow and reporting tools. FitXpress does not determine program eligibility or issue rewards.
Can employees complete biometric body measurements on their own phones?
Yes. Employees follow visual and voice instructions to capture front and side photos using a supported smartphone. Live capture does not allow selecting photos from the phone gallery. Participants who cannot use the current standing capture flow need an alternative screening method.
What privacy and security controls are available for employee wellness apps?
FitXpress supports guided live capture, scan identifiers and data processing arrangements. Where HIPAA applies to a specific deployment, 3DLOOK can act as a business associate under an executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA). GDPR-related responsibilities and international transfers depend on the deployment and applicable agreements. No general legal compliance determination is provided for employer wellness programs.
How accurate are FitXpress body measurements?
Internal validation reports 96 to 97% body-measurement accuracy against expert manual measurement, with typical absolute error of 1.5 to 2.0 cm. Typical scan-to-scan differences are under 1 cm for most evaluated measurements. Results depend on capture conditions, clothing and correct height input. Body composition outputs are estimates.
