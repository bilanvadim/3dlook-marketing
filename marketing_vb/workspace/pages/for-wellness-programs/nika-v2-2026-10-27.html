The problem

Wellness rewards depend on proof that is easy to dispute and hard to collect

Most programs verify milestones with self-reported numbers, paperwork or onsite screening events. Each source adds manual review, and none fits a workforce or membership spread across locations.

  • Self-report is unreliable

    Participants underreport weight and overreport height, often without intending to. Reward decisions built on those inputs are weak.

  • Onsite checks do not scale

    Participants question whether standards apply equally. Program owners question the data behind each payout.

Fair reward rules

Set milestone thresholds above scan-to-scan variation

A fair rule rewards real change. Repeated FitXpress scans of the same person typically differ by less than 1 cm for most evaluated measurements. A threshold set well above that range rewards measured progress, not one-off variation.

Every participant is measured with the same guided capture and the same processing, which keeps the standard the same across locations and work schedules.

Publish the rules to participants. State what qualifies, how often to scan and what happens after a retake. Clear criteria make rewards easier to administer and easier to defend.

Program integrity

A capture flow built to make submissions easier to check

When money or benefits depend on a result, the record has to hold up in a dispute or an audit. The guided flow gives reviewers more to check than a typed number or an uploaded photo.

  • Camera capture only

    Photos are taken inside the guided flow, with no upload from the phone's gallery.

  • Checks during the shot

    Real-Time Pose Validation and the Clothing Detector check pose, framing and clothing before the scan is accepted.

  • Comparable records over time

    Every checkpoint follows the same workflow, and reviewers compare each scan with earlier ones for the same participant.

  • A dated evidence trail

    Each record shows what was captured and when, which supports review of flagged submissions and later audits.

These controls support fraud review. They do not replace program rules, administrator review or the program's own audit process.

  • Security documentationA security overview is available on request. A penetration-test summary and architecture and data-flow diagrams are available under NDA. 3DLOOK is working toward a System and Organization Controls (SOC) 2 attestation report.

Frequently asked questions

Questions from benefits, operations and compliance teams

Who decides whether a participant earns the reward?

Your program. FitXpress returns measurements, estimates and capture checks. Your platform applies the milestone rules, and the program administrator makes reward and eligibility determinations.

How should we set reward thresholds?

Set thresholds well above scan-to-scan variation, which is typically under 1 cm for most evaluated measurements. State the rules to participants in advance: what qualifies, the checkpoint schedule and what happens after a retake.

How does FitXpress help with fraud?

Photos are taken inside the guided flow, with no gallery upload. Pose and clothing checks run during capture, and every checkpoint produces a comparable, dated record. These controls support review of suspicious submissions. They do not detect every form of misuse, and your program keeps its own review and audit process.

What about participants who cannot complete a scan?

The participant stands for the scan, and the current dataset does not support wheelchair users or amputees. Programs should keep an alternative way to qualify for the reward. Two gender options are available, and height, weight and gender are required inputs.

Can participants use their own phones?

Yes. Capture runs on iOS 15 or later and on Android phones, including older models, which may show a lower frame rate during capture. The participant places the phone on a stable surface and follows voice and visual prompts.

Does FitXpress support HIPAA-governed programs?

Where HIPAA applies, FitXpress can support the deployment with 3DLOOK acting as a business associate under an executed BAA. HIPAA is a regulatory framework, not a certification. Your organization determines whether its program involves protected health information.

How do results reach our reward and reporting systems?

Results return through the API as JSON once processing is complete, delivered through webhook events with polling as the documented fallback. Webhooks currently have no automatic retries or signature verification, and polling is the recommended backup. Your team or platform vendor connects the results to reward, reporting and dashboard tools. The FitXpress Admin Panel provides CSV export.

Where is data hosted?

On AWS, primarily in the US-West-2 region and partly in the US-East-1 region. For participants in the EU and UK, 3DLOOK provides a DPA with SCCs and a UK Addendum where UK GDPR applies.

Is FitXpress a medical device?

No. An independent regulatory assessment concluded that FitXpress does not meet the medical-device definition under the UK Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (UK MDR) or the EU Medical Devices Regulation (EU MDR) for its current intended purpose. FitXpress is not cleared, authorized or approved by the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA).
