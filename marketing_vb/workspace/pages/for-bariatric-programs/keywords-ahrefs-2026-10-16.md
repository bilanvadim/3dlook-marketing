## Decision

- **Primary: `bariatric patient tracking`** (US 10, CPC $0.60, commercial signal). The only follow-up-side
  phrase with measured demand, and it overlaps the hub title "Bariatric Pre-Qualification and Patient
  Progress Tracking" without repeating its head term (`bariatric pre-qualification` stays the hub's).
- Secondary: `bariatric tracking software` (10), `bariatric software` (30; GSC hub at 19.7), `muscle loss
  after bariatric surgery` (10, informational → FAQ H3).

- Pre-auth phrases have no data; they stay GEO-only (one FAQ).
- URL `/fitxpress/for-bariatric-programs/`: no keyword favours "clinics" or "programs"; "programs" names
  the actor the page writes about, matches the sibling `/fitxpress/for-glp-1-programs/` and Nika's draft,
  and the planned `for-bariatric-clinics` URL has no links pointing to it yet.
- Compliance note: "FitXpress for bariatric patient tracking" describes the program's use; the copy says
  the program links scan identifiers to patients (3DLOOK does not track people, CLAUDE.md §12).
