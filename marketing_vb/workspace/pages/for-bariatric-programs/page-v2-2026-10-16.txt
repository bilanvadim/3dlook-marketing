The problem

## What does a bariatric program lose when follow-up depends on clinic visits?

Programs accredited under the US [Metabolic and Bariatric Surgery Accreditation and Quality Improvement Program (MBSAQIP)](https://www.facs.org/quality-programs/accreditation-and-verification/metabolic-and-bariatric-surgery-accreditation-and-quality-improvement-program/standards/) follow every patient through 30 days, 6 months and 1 year, and [UK guidance from the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE)](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng246) asks for two years. Each missed time frame means documented contact attempts, and a phone check-in returns a scale weight at best.

44.6%
of patients attended follow-up five years after surgery at one German center; long distance was the most cited reason for dropping out.
[Nutrients, patients lost to follow-up after bariatric surgery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11357598/)

2 attempts
at minimum, to reach each patient who misses a time frame, each documented in the MBSAQIP registry.
[American College of Surgeons, MBSAQIP Standards](https://www.facs.org/quality-programs/accreditation-and-verification/metabolic-and-bariatric-surgery-accreditation-and-quality-improvement-program/standards/)

8.1 kg
of lean body mass is lost on average in the first 12 months after surgery, about 55% of it within 3 months.
[Obesity Reviews, lean body mass loss after bariatric surgery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9285034/)

Each patient lost to follow-up leaves a gap in the program’s outcome data, and a weight-only check cannot show lean mass loss.

Before the first consultation

## Check BMI before surgeon time is booked

At the first inquiry, the program compares BMI from the patient’s entered weight with BMI from the Smart Scales weight estimate (beta), both using entered height.

A difference above the program’s threshold goes to a coordinator for another scan, a call or an in-clinic measurement. A completed scan becomes the pre-surgery baseline.

How it works

## The scan runs inside the follow-up schedule the program already has

### Baseline before surgery

At intake, the patient completes the program’s consent flow and a guided front and side capture on their phone, with no appointment.

### Scans at home on the program’s schedule

At the program’s time frames, such as 3, 6 and 12 months, the patient scans at home and the record reaches the care team.

### Review in the program’s own system

The care team reviews change against the baseline in its electronic health record (EHR) or bariatric tracking software, or in the optional FitXpress Admin Panel with comma-separated values (CSV) export.

The recommended setup pairs the web and mobile software development kits (SDKs) for capture with the API for results ([FitXpress API documentation](https://docs.fitxpress.3dlook.me/)). The program designs consent and results screens; the capture layer stays fixed to protect measurement quality.

Accuracy and data handling

## How accurate is FitXpress for follow-up, and how is patient data handled?

For follow-up, repeatability matters most, because the care team compares each patient with their own baseline.

< 1 cmtypical scan-to-scan difference for most evaluated body measurements

96-97%accuracy against expert manual measurement, typical absolute error 1.5-2.0 cm

Both figures come from internal validation of body measurements (38-210 kg, 150-220 cm), have not been peer-reviewed, and do not cover body composition estimates or performance after bariatric surgery. The methodology is available under a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

PhotosFitXpress deletes photos immediately after processing or retains them for up to 30 days under a customer-specific policy. Retained photos are blurred, and face obfuscation is applied during capture.

Frequently asked questions

## Questions bariatric teams ask

### Does FitXpress measure muscle loss after bariatric surgery?

FitXpress does not measure muscle directly. Its lean mass estimates include organs, bone and water along with muscle, and the accuracy figures on this page do not cover body composition after surgery. Where the protocol needs a muscle measure, DXA or BIA stays the method.

### What happens when a patient cannot complete the scan?

The capture needs a smartphone and standing front and side photos; mobility, space and phone access affect completion. The program sets the retake logic and an in-clinic alternative, and the pilot measures completion before rollout.

### How does FitXpress perform at higher body weights?

Internal validation covered participants up to 210 kg, aged 16-78, in the US and Europe; performance outside that range has not been characterized. The pilot checks capture completion and measurement performance in the program’s own patients, including its heaviest.

### Can a FitXpress record support bariatric prior-authorization documentation?

A FitXpress record can supply part of it. Each insurer or health plan sets its documentation requirements and accepted sources, and some require a BMI measured at a clinical visit. The program can add dated scan outputs and the BMI inputs to its supporting records. FitXpress does not guarantee payer acceptance or approval.
