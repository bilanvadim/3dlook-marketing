## One record format for pre-qualification and follow-up

FitXpress returns each scan as a timestamped record through its application programming interface (API), with the same fields at the first scan and at every follow-up. The record works inside the patient app, care-pathway software or patient system your program already uses.

Follow-up

### Body composition estimates

Body-fat percentage, fat mass and lean mass estimates show what changed beyond weight. BMI and basal metabolic rate (BMR) are calculated metrics.

Body measurements

Follow-up

A 3D model reconstructed from each scan. On Pro, compare a follow-up scan with the pre-surgery baseline.

Pre-qualification

### Two BMI values for comparison

BMI from self-reported weight sits next to BMI from the Smart Scales weight estimate, with differences above your threshold flagged for review. Both values use the self-reported height; the program confirms height through its own process.

Capture integrity

### Quality checks and flags

Pose validation results and clothing flags are stored with each scan. The program decides whether a patient retakes the scan or is measured in clinic.

Traceability

### Timestamp and capture source

Each record shows when the values were produced and how they were captured.

FitXpress is not a medical device. It supports remote pre-qualification and follow-up; surgical candidacy and treatment decisions remain with the clinical team.

How it works

## Add body scans to the pathway your patients already follow

Your team embeds FitXpress through web and mobile software development kits (SDKs) and retrieves results through the API. Scans run inside the patient app or care-pathway software your program already uses. The FitXpress Admin Panel gives a central view of scan results, with comma-separated values (CSV) export, for teams that do not build their own dashboard.

### Scan before the first visit

After the first enquiry, the patient completes the program’s consent flow and a guided front and side capture on their phone. Real-Time Pose Validation (RTPV) guides pose and framing.

### Review before booking

The coordinator confirms the record is complete and sends it for clinical review under the program’s criteria. Flagged records go to a retake, a call or an in-clinic measurement.

### Repeat after surgery

The pre-surgery scan becomes the baseline. Patients complete follow-up scans from home on the schedule your program sets.

The recommended integration combines the SDK’s guided capture with the API for results retrieval. [3DLOOK’s mobile body scanning technology](https://3dlook.ai/technology/) explains the capture and processing approach.

Repeatability determines whether a pre-surgery scan and a follow-up scan can be compared. FitXpress’s internal validation evaluates repeated scans and comparisons with expert manual measurements.

Frequently asked questions

## Questions about remote follow-up and pre-qualification

How often can patients scan after surgery?

Your program sets the follow-up schedule. Each scan returns the same fields as the pre-surgery record, and the care team compares follow-up scans with that baseline. The program decides which results patients see.

Does FitXpress measure muscle loss after surgery?

FitXpress reports fat mass and lean mass estimates. Lean mass includes organs, bone and water along with muscle; it is not a direct measurement of skeletal muscle or muscle loss. The care team determines whether further assessment, such as dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA) or bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA), is required.

Can FitXpress run inside the program’s existing patient app?

Yes. Your team embeds guided capture through web and mobile software development kits (SDKs) and retrieves results through the FitXpress API into the patient app, care-pathway software or patient system the program already uses. The FitXpress Admin Panel gives a central view of scan results with comma-separated values (CSV) export. The [FitXpress API documentation](https://docs.fitxpress.3dlook.me/) covers integration details.

Does FitXpress decide whether a patient qualifies for surgery?

No. FitXpress supplies body metrics and discrepancy flags for review. Surgical candidacy remains the clinical team’s determination under the program’s criteria. FitXpress is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions, make treatment decisions, or determine treatment eligibility.

What happens when a patient cannot complete the scan?

The capture requires a smartphone and standing front and side photos. Mobility, available space and smartphone access affect completion. Your program sets the retake logic and an in-clinic alternative, and a pilot measures completion with and without assistance.

Has FitXpress been validated at higher body weights?

The internal validation population covered weights of 38–210 kg and heights of 150–220 cm, with participants aged 16 to 78 from the US and Europe. Performance outside this range has not been characterized. A pilot compares your patient population with this validation scope.

Can the record support prior-authorization documentation for US programs?

Each plan determines its documentation requirements and accepted sources. A FitXpress record provides a dated BMI, the height and weight used to calculate it, the source of each value and the capture method. FitXpress does not guarantee payer acceptance or approval.
