The feature explained

[H2] What is BMI verification?
BMI verification checks self-reported height and weight against additional evidence before BMI is used in a decision. FitXpress uses two guided smartphone photos to estimate weight and calculates BMI using the supplied height. The scan provides a second source for weight; height is entered separately.
A study of 2020 US survey data estimated severe obesity prevalence at 5.3% using self-reported values and 8.8% after correcting for reporting bias.

Your questions, answered

[H2] Before you
integrate.
Key distinctions for product, clinical, compliance, and underwriting teams.

Is Smart Scales a physical scale?
No. Smart Scales generates predicted weight through software. Use the measurement method required by your protocol whenever calibrated weight is necessary.

Does a mismatch mean fraud?
No. Differences can reflect reporting error, capture conditions, or model error. Your reviewers assess the context and determine what additional evidence is needed.

Does FitXpress approve eligibility or prescribing?
No. Qualified professionals retain responsibility for clinical decisions, and carriers retain responsibility for underwriting decisions.

What happens if someone cannot complete capture?
Your program defines the alternative route, which may include assisted capture or another measurement method. Consider accessibility and user support before launch.

Can we use the scan for progress tracking too?
FitXpress also provides body measurements, body-composition estimates, and 3D models. Programs can compare selected scans through a separate progress workflow. Explore body progress tracking for fitness apps.
