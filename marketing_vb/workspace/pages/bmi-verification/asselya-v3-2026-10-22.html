Additional evidence for remote review

Cross-check reported weight
before your team decides.

Remote intake often relies on a person’s reported weight. A discrepancy may require additional evidence before a pharmacy, care team, or insurer can complete review.

FitXpress adds a scan-derived weight estimate to that process. Your team determines how supplied height is verified, what differences require attention, and when a calibrated measurement is needed. Eligibility, prescribing, and underwriting remain with the responsible professionals.

Capture quality & anti-fraud support

Live capture.
No photo uploads.

Combine in-session capture, quality information, and defined review rules. No single check establishes identity or excludes every manipulation attempt.

Live capture is required

Users must take both photos live inside the standard capture flow. Uploading existing photos from a camera roll or file is not supported. This removes the standard route for submitting previously saved or edited photos.

Pose and clothing information

RTPV requires alignment checks. Clothing-related flags support review; the Clothing Detector does not automatically request a clothing change or rescan.

Discrepancy review

Compare reported and predicted values alongside capture-quality information. Account for expected model error before setting an escalation threshold.

Your questions, answered

Before you
integrate.

Key distinctions for product, clinical, compliance, and underwriting teams.

Can users upload saved photos?

No. The standard capture flow requires users to take both photos live. Camera-roll and file uploads are unavailable. Live capture supports anti-fraud review; it does not establish identity or rule out every manipulation attempt.

What does “BMI verification” mean here?

A cross-check between self-reported data and scan-derived predicted weight, with BMI calculated using supplied height. FitXpress does not independently measure height in this flow.

Is Smart Scales a physical scale?

No. Smart Scales generates predicted weight through software. Use the measurement method required by your protocol whenever calibrated weight is necessary.

Does a mismatch mean fraud?

No. Differences can reflect reporting error, capture conditions, or model error. Your reviewers assess the context and determine what additional evidence is needed.

Does FitXpress approve eligibility or prescribing?

No. FitXpress is not a medical device. Qualified professionals retain responsibility for clinical decisions, and carriers retain responsibility for underwriting decisions.

What happens if someone cannot complete capture?

Your program defines the alternative route, which may include assisted capture or another measurement method. Consider accessibility and user support before launch.

Can we use the scan for progress tracking too?

FitXpress also provides body measurements, body-composition estimates, and 3D models. Your program can compare selected scans through a separate progress workflow.
