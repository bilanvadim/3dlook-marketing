The problem

What does weight-only tracking leave out of GLP-1 follow-up?

Scale weight shows how much a patient lost. It does not show how much of that loss was fat and how much was lean mass.

Outputs and capabilities

FitXpress returns a timestamped record through its application programming interface (API). Your program chooses which body metrics patients and care teams can access.

Estimated and calculated metrics

Composition estimates and calculated metrics

Body-fat percentage, fat mass and lean mass are estimates. BMI and basal metabolic rate (BMR) are calculated metrics. The care team reviews these outputs alongside entered weight.

How it works

Add guided body scanning to your program’s existing check-in schedule

Your team embeds FitXpress through web and mobile software development kits (SDKs), including supported iOS and Android integrations, and retrieves results through the API.

  1. Capture a baseline

    The patient completes the program’s consent flow and takes a guided scan on their phone at intake.

  2. Repeat on your schedule

    Real-Time Pose Validation (RTPV) provides pose and framing guidance during front and side photo capture at each scheduled check-in.

  3. Review and discuss progress

    Your program links each scan identifier to the patient’s record. The care team reviews results, and your app displays the progress views selected by the program.

The recommended integration combines the SDK’s guided capture with the API for results retrieval. 3DLOOK’s mobile body scanning technology explains the capture and processing approach.

Understand measurement performance and patient data handling

Repeatability is a key measure for comparing body measurements across check-ins. FitXpress’s internal validation evaluates repeated scans and comparisons with expert manual measurements.

Body scanning accuracy, repeatability and validation methodology

  • Photo retentionFitXpress deletes photos immediately after processing or retains them for up to 30 days under a customer-specific policy. Retained photos are blurred, and face obfuscation is applied during capture.
  • Scan identifiersScan records use randomly generated identifiers. Your program links these identifiers to its patient records.
  • Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)FitXpress can support HIPAA-governed deployments where 3DLOOK acts as a business associate under an executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)In most enterprise deployments, the customer acts as the data controller and 3DLOOK acts as the data processor under GDPR. These roles are defined in the deployment’s contractual terms.
  • EncryptionData is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Model training3DLOOK does not use production customer data to train its models without the customer’s explicit, documented authorization.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about remote progress tracking

How can a GLP-1 program track body composition remotely?

FitXpress returns body composition estimates and 80+ body measurements from two photos captured on a patient’s phone through the program’s app. Your program defines the check-in schedule and which results patients and care teams can access.

Can a program show progress inside its existing patient app?

Yes. Your team can display dated body measurements and body composition estimates in its existing app using results returned through the FitXpress API. The Pro plan also includes 3D Body Progress to compare selected scans. Your program controls the patient-facing views and which results the care team reviews.

Does FitXpress measure muscle loss?

FitXpress reports fat mass and lean mass estimates. Lean mass includes organs, bone and water along with muscle; it is not a direct measurement of skeletal muscle or muscle loss. The care team determines whether further assessment is required.

Does FitXpress replace DXA or clinic body composition checks?

FitXpress supports remote progress tracking and clinician review. Programs retain DXA, clinic BIA, or calibrated scales wherever their protocols require them. FitXpress is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions, make treatment decisions, or determine treatment eligibility.

Can FitXpress also support weight and BMI cross-checks?

Yes. Smart Scales provides a weight estimate from the scan. Your care team can compare that estimate with entered weight using review thresholds defined by your program. Prescription eligibility decisions remain with the prescriber. Weight and BMI verification describes this workflow.

How are results delivered?

Results are retrieved through the FitXpress API, including structured body data, processing status and timestamps. Your program links scan identifiers to its patient records and builds the views required for follow-up. The FitXpress API documentation covers integration details.
