Data handling
## How patient body data is handled
In most deployments, your program is the data controller and 3DLOOK processes data on your behalf.
### Photos
Deleted immediately after processing or within 30 days, depending on your configuration. Faces are obfuscated at capture, and any stored photo is auto-blurred.
### Identifiers
No personal identifiers are processed. Scans link to the identifier your program supplies, and 3DLOOK can't identify an individual from stored records.
### Encryption and region
Transport Layer Security (TLS) in transit. Amazon Simple Storage Service (Amazon S3) server-side encryption at rest, always on. Data is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the US. No European region is available today.
### HIPAA
Pending confirmation
For deployments governed by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), 3DLOOK acts as a business associate under an executed business associate agreement (BAA).
### GDPR
3DLOOK acts as processor under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). A data processing agreement (DPA) with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) covers international transfers, with a UK Addendum where UK GDPR applies.
### Model training
Production patient data isn't used to train the models without your explicit, documented authorization.
### Retention and deletion
Measurement and composition records are kept until your agreement says otherwise. Deletion runs by scan ID on request. Self-serve deletion isn't available today.

FAQ
## Questions GLP-1 and weight management teams ask
Will a scan step slow down check-ins?
Capture takes two guided photos, and processing takes under 45 seconds. Real-Time Pose Validation corrects framing during the shot, which reduces retakes. The scan runs inside a flow your program already has.
Does FitXpress replace DXA or clinic body composition checks?
No. DXA and clinic BIA remain the reference methods at milestone visits where your protocol requires them. FitXpress covers remote intake and the check-ins between visits.
Does FitXpress measure muscle loss?
No. Lean mass is an estimate of body mass excluding fat, and it includes organs, bone and water along with muscle. FitXpress reports fat mass and lean mass estimates next to weight for the care team to review.
Can FitXpress confirm BMI eligibility for a prescription?
Eligibility decisions stay with the prescriber. For BMI checks inside the order flow, see 
FitXpress for BMI verification.
Does it work for patients with higher body weight?
The validation population covers 38 to 210 kg. The patient must stand throughout the scan, and the current dataset doesn't support wheelchair users or amputees. Two gender options are available. Accuracy is not published by body shape.
How often should patients scan?
Your protocol sets the schedule. Most programs scan at intake and at fixed check-ins. FitXpress is built to show meaningful change over weeks, and very small day-to-day changes fall within scan-to-scan variation.
What does the patient see, and what does the care team see?
Your program decides. Results can appear in the patient's app, stay with the care team, or both. The Admin Panel gives your team a central view of scan results.
Is FitXpress cleared by the FDA?
FitXpress has not been cleared, authorized or approved by the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA). An independent regulatory assessment found it falls outside the UK and EU medical-device definitions for its current intended purpose. Whether your complete workflow needs FDA review depends on how your program uses the outputs.
Do you sign a business associate agreement?
A BAA is available during contracting for qualifying enterprise deployments. Whether HIPAA applies depends on your program's status and whether protected health information is involved.
Is 3DLOOK SOC 2 certified?
Not yet. 3DLOOK has completed an initial readiness assessment against the System and Organization Controls (SOC) 2 Trust Services Criteria and is working toward an attestation report. Security documentation, including a penetration-test summary, is available under NDA.
How do results reach our systems, and what are the known gaps?
Results arrive through webhook events in JavaScript Object Notation (JSON), with comma-separated values (CSV) and 3D model export. Webhooks have no automatic retries or signature verification today, and polling is the documented backup. Evaluation runs through a demo and a trial account, and there is no separate sandbox.
