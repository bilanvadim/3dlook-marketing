The problem

What does a GLP-1 program miss when it tracks only weight?

Most patients stop GLP-1 treatment within a year. While they stay, a weight entered at home shows how much a patient lost, but not how much of that loss was fat and how much was lean mass.

64.8%

of US adults without type 2 diabetes who started a GLP-1 medication stopped within one year. Greater weight loss was associated with staying on treatment.

JAMA Network Open, "Discontinuation and Reinitiation of Dual-Labeled GLP-1 Receptor Agonists Among US Adults With Overweight or Obesity"
About 25%

of the weight participants lost in a 72-week trial was lean mass, and about 75% was fat mass, measured by dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA).

Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism, "Body composition changes during weight reduction with tirzepatide in the SURMOUNT-1 study"
34%

of US employers with 200 or more workers that cover GLP-1s for weight loss require a dietitian, case manager, therapist or lifestyle program for coverage.

KFF, 2025 Employer Health Benefits Survey

A 2025 joint advisory from four US obesity and nutrition societies includes body composition assessment in the exam before GLP-1 therapy. Between clinic or DXA visits, a remote program that tracks only weight has one number per check-in to work with. For a program paid per active patient, each patient who stops in the first year is revenue it does not earn back.

How it works

The scan runs inside the check-ins the protocol already has

A GLP-1 prescription platform, telehealth program or coaching app embeds FitXpress through web and mobile software development kits (SDKs), including supported iOS and Android integrations.

  1. The patient scans at intake.

    The first scan sets the baseline on the patient's own phone, after the program's own consent screen.

  2. Real-Time Pose Validation (RTPV) guides each check-in.

    The patient takes two photos at home on the protocol's schedule, and RTPV gives pose and framing guidance during capture.

  3. The care team reviews the change.

    FitXpress returns the record through the API, and the program links each scan identifier to the patient's record. The patient sees the comparison in the app, and the clinician or coach reviews the same record.

Recommended setup: the SDK together with the API, because pose and framing validation runs in the SDK's capture layer. How the technology works →

Proof and data handling

How accurate is FitXpress for progress tracking, and how is patient data handled?

For progress tracking, repeatability is the figure that matters. For most evaluated measurements, repeated scans showed typical scan-to-scan differences of less than 1 cm. The program compares each patient with their own earlier scans, which makes repeatability more relevant here than absolute accuracy.

< 1 cmtypical scan-to-scan difference for most measurements
96-97%accuracy against expert manual measurement, typical absolute error 1.5-2.0 cm

These figures come from internal validation of body measurements. Body composition values are estimates derived from the measurements with height and, where provided, weight. Peer review and third-party clinical certification are not part of that record, and the full methodology is available under a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).   • Photos. FitXpress deletes photos immediately after processing or retains them for up to 30 days under a customer-specific policy. Retained photos are blurred, and face obfuscation is applied during capture.
  • Identifiers. Scan records use anonymized, randomly generated identifiers.
  • HIPAA. FitXpress can support HIPAA-governed deployments where 3DLOOK acts as a business associate under an executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
  • GDPR. In most enterprise deployments, the customer acts as the data controller and 3DLOOK acts as the data processor under GDPR.
  • Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Model training. 3DLOOK does not use production customer data to train its models without the customer's explicit, documented authorization.

FAQ

Questions GLP-1 and weight management teams ask

How can a GLP-1 weight-loss clinic track patient body composition remotely?

With a smartphone scan at each check-in. FitXpress returns body composition estimates and 80+ body measurements from two photos the patient takes at home, inside the program's app. Remote options are compared in 7 body composition tools for remote GLP-1 clinics.

Does FitXpress measure muscle loss?

No. Lean mass is an estimate of body mass excluding fat, and it includes organs, bone and water along with muscle. FitXpress reports fat mass and lean mass estimates next to weight for the care team to review. More in lean body mass vs muscle mass.

Does FitXpress replace DXA or clinic body composition checks?

No. DXA and clinic BIA remain the reference methods at milestone visits. FitXpress covers remote intake and the check-ins between them.

Can FitXpress confirm BMI eligibility for a prescription?

Eligibility decisions stay with the prescriber. For BMI checks inside the order flow, see FitXpress for BMI verification.

How are results delivered?

Results are retrieved through the FitXpress API. The response contains structured body data, processing status and timestamps. Integration details are covered in the FitXpress API documentation.
