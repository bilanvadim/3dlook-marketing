## Runners-up

- Obesity, Gasoyan et al. 2025 (Cleveland Clinic): of 7,881 adults without T2D on semaglutide or tirzepatide (2021-2023), 20.4% stopped within 3 months and 32.0% between 3 and 12 months; one-year weight loss 3.6% / 6.8% / 11.9%. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12381620/ (one health system; stopped = gap >90 days)
- Prime Therapeutics, 25 Jun 2025: one-year persistence on Wegovy/Zepbound rose from 33.2% (2021 starters) to 62.6% (2024 starters); 8.1% still on treatment at 3 years (2021 starters, claims only). https://www.primetherapeutics.com/documents/d/primetherapeutics/prime-therapeutics_glp-1-therapy-to-treat-obesity-among-members-without-diabetes_three-year-persistence
- STEP 1 extension (DOM 2022): participants regained two-thirds of prior weight loss one year after stopping (n=327). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9542252/
- UK, for the BMI verification page, not this one: GPhC distance-pharmacy guidance (Feb 2025) requires independent verification of weight, height and/or BMI; GPhC review (Apr 2026): 1,307 concerns Jan 2024-Dec 2025, inadequate BMI verification third most reported (11%). It also lists easily manipulated photographs and requests for photos in tight-fitting clothes as public concerns.

## Do not claim

- That patients quit because they cannot see progress: 1.7% stopped over unsatisfactory weight loss, 47.6% over cost or insurance, 14.6% side effects, 11.8% shortages (Gasoyan, Obesity 2025, n=288, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12636059/).
- That FitXpress measures muscle, muscle loss or sarcopenia, or preserves lean mass.
- That GLP-1s "destroy muscle" (placebo showed the same split).
- That the advisory endorses phone scanning, or that FitXpress meets GPhC requirements.
- "43% of employers cover GLP-1s" (5,000+ firms only) or "1 in 12" as today's persistence.
