The problem

## What does weight-only tracking leave out of GLP-1 follow-up?

Scale weight shows how much a patient lost. It does not show how much of that loss was fat and how much was lean mass.

64.8%
of adults without type 2 diabetes stopped GLP-1 medication within one year in a US study.
[JAMA Network Open study of GLP-1 discontinuation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11786232/). The figure describes the study population.

About 25%
of weight lost was lean mass and about 75% was fat mass, measured by dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA) in a SURMOUNT-1 substudy.
[Body composition changes during weight reduction with tirzepatide](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11965027/). Individual changes vary.

A 
[joint advisory on nutritional priorities for GLP-1 therapy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12304835/) includes body composition assessment before treatment. Between clinic or DXA visits, a remote program that tracks only weight cannot follow body composition.

[GLP-1 market growth and patient progress tracking](https://3dlook.ai/content-hub/glp-1-market/) provides context for program design.

Outputs and capabilities

## Body data and progress capabilities for your program

FitXpress returns a timestamped record through its application programming interface (API). Your program chooses which body metrics patients and care teams can access.

Body measurements

### 80+ body measurements
Waist, hip, chest, thigh, calf and other body dimensions provide a record of change in centimeters across check-ins.

Estimated and calculated metrics

### Composition estimates and calculated metrics
Body-fat percentage, fat mass and lean mass are estimates. BMI and basal metabolic rate (BMR) are calculated metrics. The care team reviews these outputs alongside entered weight.

Weight cross-checks

### Smart Scales weight estimate
Your care team compares the estimate with entered weight using review thresholds defined by your program.

Body shape

### 3D body model
The model represents body shape reconstructed from the guided scan. Your team determines how the model is used in the app.

Pro plan

### 3D Body Progress
Compare two selected scans to present changes in body shape over time. Your program controls the dates selected and the patient-facing view.

Record management

### Processing status and timestamps
Your program retrieves processing status, identifies completed results and orders scan records by date.

FitXpress is not a medical device. It supports remote progress tracking and clinician review; treatment decisions remain with the care team.

Validation and data handling

## Understand measurement performance and patient data handling

Repeatability is a key measure for comparing body measurements across check-ins. FitXpress’s internal validation evaluates repeated scans and comparisons with expert manual measurements.

< 1 cm
Typical scan-to-scan difference for most evaluated body measurements.

96–97%
Body-measurement accuracy against expert manual measurement, with typical absolute error of 1.5–2.0 cm.

These body-measurement figures come from internal validation and have not been peer-reviewed. Body composition values are estimates. Supporting methodology is available under a non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

[Body scanning accuracy, repeatability and validation methodology](https://3dlook.ai/content-hub/mobile-body-scanning-accuracy/)

Photo retention
FitXpress deletes photos immediately after processing or retains them for up to 30 days under a customer-specific policy. Retained photos are blurred, and face obfuscation is applied during capture.

Scan identifiers
Scan records use randomly generated identifiers. Your program links these identifiers to its patient records.

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)
FitXpress can support HIPAA-governed deployments where 3DLOOK acts as a business associate under an executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
In most enterprise deployments, the customer acts as the data controller and 3DLOOK acts as the data processor under GDPR. These roles are defined in the deployment’s contractual terms.

Encryption
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Model training
3DLOOK does not use production customer data to train its models without the customer’s explicit, documented authorization.

Frequently asked questions

## Questions about remote progress tracking

How can a GLP-1 program track body composition remotely?
FitXpress returns body composition estimates and 80+ body measurements from two photos captured on a patient’s phone through the program’s app. Your program defines the check-in schedule and which results patients and care teams can access.

Can a program show progress inside its existing patient app?
Yes. Your team can display dated body measurements and body composition estimates in its existing app using results returned through the FitXpress API. The Pro plan also includes 3D Body Progress to compare selected scans. Your program controls the patient-facing views and which results the care team reviews.

Does FitXpress measure muscle loss?
FitXpress reports fat mass and lean mass estimates. 
[Lean mass includes organs, bone and water along with muscle](https://3dlook.ai/content-hub/lean-body-mass-vs-muscle-mass/); it is not a direct measurement of skeletal muscle or muscle loss. The care team determines whether further assessment is required.

Does FitXpress replace DXA or clinic body composition checks?
FitXpress supports remote progress tracking and clinician review. Programs retain DXA, clinic BIA, or calibrated scales wherever their protocols require them. FitXpress is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions, make treatment decisions, or determine treatment eligibility.

Can FitXpress also support weight and BMI cross-checks?
Yes. Smart Scales provides a weight estimate from the scan. Your care team can compare that estimate with entered weight using review thresholds defined by your program. Prescription eligibility decisions remain with the prescriber. 
[Weight and BMI verification](https://3dlook.ai/for-bmi-verification/) describes this workflow.

How are results delivered?
Results are retrieved through the FitXpress API, including structured body data, processing status and timestamps. Your program links scan identifiers to its patient records and builds the views required for follow-up. The 
[FitXpress API documentation](https://docs.fitxpress.3dlook.me/) covers integration details.
