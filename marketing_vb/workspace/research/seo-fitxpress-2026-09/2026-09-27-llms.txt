# 3DLOOK

> 3DLOOK provides AI-powered mobile body scanning technology that turns guided smartphone photos into structured body measurements, 3D body models, body composition outputs, and body progress data for business use cases across fitness, wellness, telehealth, GLP-1 and weight management, insurance, occupational health, clinical trials, apparel, uniforms, made-to-measure and on-demand manufacturing.
