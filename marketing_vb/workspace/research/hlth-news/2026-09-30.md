🔥 [3/3] Enveda Raises $311 Million to Advance AI-Discovered Medicines

Ендпоінти ENV-308 у статті не названі.

  Кут: Після раунду на $311M ENV-308 іде в пізніші фази як засіб утримати вагу після GLP-1, а такі дослідження тримаються на повторних вимірах тіла. Пропонуємо at-home скан за 2 фото між візитами: BMI, body composition і виміри тіла зі структурованим audit-ready записом, без зайвих поїздок на сайт.

A GLP-1 program doesn't end at the last dose anymore.

Enveda has closed a $311 million Series E. Its metabolic health candidate, ENV-308, is being developed to support weight-loss maintenance after patients discontinue GLP-1 medicines.

It's early. The study ran in 88 healthy volunteers, and later-stage trials come next.

Don't wait for the drug. Treat life after GLP-1 as its own phase now.

The one change I'd make: record weight, waist and body composition at the last dose, not only at intake. Maintenance is judged against where the patient finished. Without that record, you can't tell maintenance from slow regain.

Then book check-ins for the months after, when patients go quiet. A smartphone body scan like FitXpress covers that stretch without a clinic visit.

What do you record at the last dose, and how many weeks until you see that patient's data again?

Hims & Hers' weight loss AI is really a bet on the patient record.

It has rolled out its AI-native care platform to Hims weight loss members. Scheduling, lifestyle and admin chats get automated. Dose adjustments, persistent side effects and treatment changes stay with licensed providers.

The useful part is the persistent patient context. If a member reports nausea, the system checks it against a recent dose increase and previous symptoms. The clinician gets that history in a structured summary.

That summary is only as good as its weakest field. If weigh-ins are self-reported and patchy, the trend behind a dose change is partly guesswork.

Before automating triage, I'd list every field feeding escalation. Next to each: its source and how stale it gets. Then I'd fix the stalest input before touching the model.
