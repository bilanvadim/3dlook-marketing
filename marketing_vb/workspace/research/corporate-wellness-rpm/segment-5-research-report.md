# Segment Five Corporate Wellness and Remote Patient Monitoring Wrist Wearables

## Scope and filter

For Segment 5, I used the research plan’s strict logic: this bucket is about **B2B deployments at scale** where wearables are shipped or operationalized for employees, patients, trial participants, or insured members, and where **wrist measurement is a must-have because one bad sizing process becomes a fleet-wide operational problem**. The plan also says to exclude Meta-list companies themselves, keep only cases with active US and/or EU sales, and move unclear names to **Needs verification** instead of forcing them into the main tab. fileciteturn0file0

For this segment, I treated a company as a strong fit when public materials showed at least one of the following: a **wrist watch / bracelet / band used in RPM**, a **clinical-trial platform explicitly built around wearables used on the body in real life**, or an **employer / payer program that operationalizes device-verified activity or wearable-linked rewards at scale**. In RPM and medical-watch programs, correct fit matters because loose wear can reduce stable sensor-to-skin contact and degrade continuous wrist vitals collection; in decentralized trials, vendors repeatedly emphasize continuous data quality, participant compliance, and wearability; in employer and payer programs, verified steps / heart-rate activity from linked wearables drive rewards, which turns bad wrist fit into a support, adherence, and returns problem across the whole population. citeturn6search0turn6search2turn7search4turn24search6turn25search5
