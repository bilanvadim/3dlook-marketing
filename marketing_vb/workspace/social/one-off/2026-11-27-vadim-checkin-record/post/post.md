I'd design a fitness app's check-in backwards, starting from the progress review.

Members log weight on some weeks, skip others, and add a quick note later. At week eight, a coach or the member looks back to see what changed. Mixed formats make that review hard to read.

Start there. Decide what the week-eight review needs on one screen. Then make every check-in capture exactly that, in the same fields, on the member profile.

FitXpress fits that check-in. Two guided smartphone photos return 80+ body measurements and body composition estimates in under 45 seconds. Each scan comes back as one timestamped record, and your platform chooses what the member and the coach see.

FitXpress is not a medical device and doesn't replace a coach.
