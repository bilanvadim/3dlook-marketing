Meet Lotessa 👋 (that's the logo above), now live on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Lotessa is a wellness platform built for adults on a GLP-1 journey. It brings medication and side-effect tracking, progress charts, non-scale victories, education, and a supportive community into one place.

Through our partnership, FitXpress by 3DLOOK adds another way to see progress: a 3D body scan taken with your smartphone, so you can track changes in measurements and body shape alongside the number on the scale.

Because progress isn't only what the scale says. 📲
