Lotessa, a 3DLOOK partner, is now available on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Congratulations to Tsitsi Chinyandura and the Lotessa team on the launch. Lotessa is a UK digital wellness and self-management platform built for adults using injectable or oral GLP-1 medication for obesity treatment. It brings medication and side-effect tracking, progress charts, non-scale victories, education, and a moderated community into one place.

Through the partnership, FitXpress by 3DLOOK supports Lotessa's progress tracking: users can take a 3D body scan with a smartphone to follow changes in measurements and body shape, a view of progress that weight on its own does not always capture.

On a GLP-1 journey, the scale is one signal. Measurements and shape are another, alongside the medication tracking, education, and community Lotessa already brings together.
