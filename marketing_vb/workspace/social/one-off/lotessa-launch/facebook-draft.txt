Lotessa, a 3DLOOK partner, is now available on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Lotessa is a UK digital wellness and self-management platform for adults using injectable or oral GLP-1 medication for obesity treatment. It brings together medication and side-effect tracking, progress charts, non-scale victories, education, and a moderated community, all in one app.

FitXpress by 3DLOOK complements the platform by giving users a way to track physical progress alongside everything else: a 3D body scan taken with a smartphone shows changes in measurements and body shape, a view the scale alone doesn't provide.

Medication tracking, education, community, and progress monitoring, now in one place.
