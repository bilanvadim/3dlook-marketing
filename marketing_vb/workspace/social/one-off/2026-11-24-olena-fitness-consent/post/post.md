Asking members for body scan photos in a fitness app fails when they cannot see where those images go.

When a scan opens a separate app under another brand, or hides retention terms in legal text, many members close the screen.

Trust starts on the consent screen. Say there, in plain words, where the photos go and when they are deleted.

Under GDPR, in most enterprise deployments, your app acts as the data controller.

FitXpress runs inside your app, under your brand, through an SDK and API. Your team controls the consent wording and results view. Two guided phone photos return 80+ body measurements and body composition estimates in under 45 seconds.

Photos are deleted after processing or kept up to 30 days under your policy. Faces are obscured at capture, and kept photos are blurred.

FitXpress is not a medical device and doesn’t replace a coach.

What share of members complete the photo consent step on their first try in your app?
