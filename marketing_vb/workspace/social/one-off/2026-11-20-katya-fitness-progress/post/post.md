Weight alone makes real progress look like noise.

Take someone twelve weeks into a program. Waist down 2.5 cm. Body fat down 2%. The scale moved 300 grams.

If weight is the only number on the screen, they read that as failure. The app has nothing else to show them.

My rule of thumb for progress features: show members the numbers that move on a 4 to 12 week clock, at each check-in. More frequent logging only adds more noise.

That's the job FitXpress does inside a fitness app. Two guided phone photos return 80+ body measurements and body composition estimates in under 45 seconds. Members compare check-ins in your app, under your brand.

It isn't a medical device and doesn't replace a coach.
