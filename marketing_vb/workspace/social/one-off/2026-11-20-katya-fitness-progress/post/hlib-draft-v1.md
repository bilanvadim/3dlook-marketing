Most fitness apps lose members between month one and month three because progress looks like noise.

A user trains for 12 weeks, drops an inch off their waist, and shifts body fat by 2% - while total weight barely moves by half a pound. If the app only logs weight, the user assumes the program failed.

My rule of thumb for progress features: stop asking users to track more often, and start showing changes that actually move on a 4 to 12 week clock.

Inside the app, FitXpress turns two phone photos into a structured record in under 45 seconds - circumferences, body composition estimates, and a side-by-side comparison kept under your own brand. It is not a medical device and does not replace a coach.

What progress metric do your members actually check after month two? 💭
