A paid tier that only tracks weight is hard to sell.

If premium repeats what the free log already does, the upgrade feels thin. The member sees the same chart behind a price.

My test for a premium feature: does it show something the free log can't? A side-by-side 3D view of two scans passes. Another weight chart doesn't.

In FitXpress, two guided smartphone photos return 80+ body measurements and body composition estimates in under 45 seconds. On the FitXpress Pro plan, your app also gets 3D Body Progress tracking and 3D Goal Visualization. The first compares two scans as 3D models. The second builds a 3D body model at a target weight from a scan. Both fit a premium tier.

FitXpress is not a medical device and doesn't replace a coach.

What share of your active users upgrade to a paid plan today?
