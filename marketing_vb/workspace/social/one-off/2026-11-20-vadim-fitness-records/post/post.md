A progress view is only as reliable as the records behind it.

Here's what three months of check-ins can look like in a fitness app. Week 1 is a tape measurement at the navel. Week 6 is taken a few centimetres higher. Week 12 is a coach note that says "looks leaner".

None of them compare. When the scale barely moves and the body is changing anyway, the app has nothing solid to show the member.

The fix I'd push for is boring. Every check-in in the same structured format, from the same guided capture.

That's how FitXpress works. Two guided smartphone photos return 80+ body measurements and body composition estimates in under 45 seconds. Each scan comes back as one timestamped record with the same structure, whether it's week 1 or week 12. FitXpress is not a medical device and doesn't replace a coach.

How many formats does one member's check-in history sit in on your platform today?
