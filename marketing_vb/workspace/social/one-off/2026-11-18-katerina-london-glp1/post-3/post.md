Many GLP-1 weight checks still end with someone watching a video.

A patient films the scale reading or joins a video call, and a pharmacist checks it on screen.

Both help. Each also costs clinical time on every patient, and that cost grows with every new prescription.

I'd change the order of the work. Capture the photos live inside the consultation, with a prompted hand raise and a clothing check. Compare an estimated weight with the declared one against your own threshold. Then put the reviewer's attention on the cases above it. Your clinicians still decide for every patient.

From the photos to structured results takes under 45 seconds.
