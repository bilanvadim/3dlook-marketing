Every GLP-1 approval should leave a clear record of how the weight was confirmed.

The GPhC's 2025 distance-selling guidance asks prescribers to verify weight, height and/or BMI independently. An online questionnaire alone doesn't meet that bar.

A defensible record answers four things. When the photos were taken. Whether they were captured live, with a prompted hand raise, or uploaded later. Whether clothing hid the body, and how the declared weight compares with an independent estimate. Then the threshold the pharmacy set, and what its clinician decided.

A photo in a folder carries none of that. A FitXpress session record carries the first three, and the clinician adds the fourth. The photos can be deleted afterwards. The record stays.

One online pharmacy already runs this as the BMI step in its checkout.
