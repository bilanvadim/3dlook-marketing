Three online pharmacies approved GLP-1 medication for a reporter using AI-edited photos.

That was ITV News in January. A GPhC spokesperson responded: "having one photo by itself is not going to be sufficient."

I wasn't surprised. In March I asked two free AI tools to make me 27 kg heavier. Both took seconds, and both could pass a quick look.

The prescriber's question has changed. It is less "does this photo look right?" and more "was it taken live, just now?"

A camera-roll upload can't answer that. A capture inside the consultation can. There's no upload button. The patient raises a hand on request, which helps show a live person is on camera. Loose clothing gets flagged, and an estimated weight is compared with the declared one.
