A GLP-1 program doesn't end at the last dose anymore.

Enveda has closed a $311 million Series E. Its metabolic health candidate, ENV-308, is being developed to support weight-loss maintenance after patients discontinue GLP-1 medicines.

It's early. The study ran in 88 healthy volunteers, and later-stage trials come next.

Don't wait for the drug. Treat life after GLP-1 as its own phase now.

The one change I'd make: record weight, waist and body composition at the last dose, not only at intake. Maintenance is judged against where the patient finished. Without that record, you can't tell maintenance from slow regain.

Then book check-ins for the months after, when patients go quiet. A smartphone body scan like FitXpress covers that stretch without a clinic visit.

What do you record at the last dose, and how many weeks until you see that patient's data again?
