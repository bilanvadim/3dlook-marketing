Counsel Health's $25-a-month weight program lives or dies on its check-ins.

They launched it this week with LillyDirect, Eli Lilly's direct-to-consumer pharmacy. After digital intake and a physician evaluation, eligible patients can be prescribed obesity medication.

Between visits, AI check-ins monitor dose titration and tolerability and flag side effects that need a doctor. The membership buys unlimited physician access within about 15 minutes.

Founder Dr. Muthu Alagappan's line: "a prescription alone is not a care model."

At that price, unlimited physician access only holds if most check-ins never need one. That makes the escalation rules the product.

I'd go rules first, cadence second. Decide which symptoms and dose changes send a patient to a physician. Then set the cadence by how fast each signal moves. A weekly "how are you feeling?" with no trigger behind it is just chat.

In your weight program, what share of check-ins end with a clinician today?
