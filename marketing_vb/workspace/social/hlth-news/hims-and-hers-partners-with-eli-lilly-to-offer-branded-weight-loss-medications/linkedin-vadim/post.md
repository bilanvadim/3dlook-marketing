Hims & Hers just chose lower margins over compounded GLP-1s.

It now offers Eli Lilly's Zepbound and oral Foundayo directly through its platform. Self-pay starts at $149 a month for Foundayo and $299 for Zepbound. A similar Novo Nordisk deal came earlier this year.

Branded drugs typically carry thinner margins. The care around them doesn't get cheaper. Every patient still needs a virtual consultation, then regular check-ins and clinical support.

That's where I'd look first. Count the clinician minutes spent confirming facts the patient could have entered before the call. Think weight, height and current medications. On a thin-margin program, each of those minutes is a cost line.

The order matters. Structured intake first, then the consult. The clinician then spends the call on the prescribing decision.

Body data at that step is what we build FitXpress for.
