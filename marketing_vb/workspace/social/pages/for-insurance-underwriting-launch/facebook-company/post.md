When a life insurance application relies on self-reported height and weight, the insurer has to decide how much to trust two typed numbers.

Munich Re's misclassification analysis names build and BMI as the top reason applications are misclassified in accelerated underwriting. A paramedical exam measures them on site, but it is a scheduled visit that takes days to weeks.

FitXpress, from 3DLOOK, adds a faster step inside the online application. The applicant takes two guided photos with a smartphone. In under 45 seconds, the insurer's underwriting team gets a predicted weight to compare with the one that was entered, along with a flag when the difference crosses the limit the insurer has set. In the recommended setup, the applicant sees a short "Scan submitted" message, and the results go only to the insurer.

FitXpress is not a medical device. Underwriters decide eligibility, risk class and pricing, and where an insurer's rules require an exam, the exam stays.
