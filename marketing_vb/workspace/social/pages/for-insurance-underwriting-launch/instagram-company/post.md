A life insurance application can check disclosed build without adding an appointment.

When someone applies online, height and weight usually arrive as two typed numbers. According to Munich Re, build and BMI are the top misclassification reason in accelerated underwriting.

FitXpress adds a short step inside the application. The applicant takes two guided photos with a smartphone, with live pose and framing guidance. In under 45 seconds, the insurer's underwriting team sees a predicted weight next to the one that was entered, and a flag when the gap crosses the threshold the insurer set.

In the recommended setup, the applicant just sees "Scan submitted". The underwriters keep the decisions.
