Munich Re's misclassification analysis names build and BMI as the top misclassification reason in accelerated underwriting.

Gen Re's Next Gen Underwriting Survey reports that 59% of applications qualify for accelerated underwriting, and LIMRA puts automated programs at 9 days against 27 for traditional underwriting.

The speed is worth keeping. The weak point is build, because the application still relies on self-reported height and weight.

FitXpress adds a second source of build evidence inside the e-application, without adding an appointment. The applicant takes two guided smartphone photos. In under 45 seconds, the underwriting team receives predicted weight, scan-derived BMI and a disclosure flag measured against the carrier's own threshold. Disclosed and scan-derived BMI use the same supplied height, and the result arrives as one timestamped record that can return to the existing review workflow.

The carrier's rules decide what happens next: cases below the threshold can remain on the accelerated path, and flagged cases go to the existing review queue. Where guidelines call for a paramedical exam, the exam stays. FitXpress is not a medical device and does not make underwriting decisions.

Teams can start with a shadow evaluation: a defined cohort scans while outputs stay out of underwriting decisions, and results are compared with the carrier's reference evidence on criteria agreed in advance. 📋
