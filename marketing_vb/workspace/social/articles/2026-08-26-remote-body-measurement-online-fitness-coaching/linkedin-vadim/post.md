Every remote measurement workflow needs a fallback, and most teams skip it.

Some clients can't meet the standard capture requirements. Poor lighting, no floor space, limited mobility. If there's no alternative, that check-in just doesn't happen, and the client record has a hole in it.

The fix is two decisions made before launch. First, define the alternative method: trained manual measurement, a scale reading, or an in-person assessment. Second, record which method produced each result.

That second part gets missed. A mobile scan and a tape reading aren't interchangeable. If you mix them in one trend without a flag, you're reading measurement variation as progress.

I'd make the capture method a required field on every record. It costs nothing at setup and saves a messy audit later.
