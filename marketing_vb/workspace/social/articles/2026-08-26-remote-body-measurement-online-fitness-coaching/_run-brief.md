## Claims discipline for this article

Numbers and boundaries a post may state, in the article's live wording only.
Anything not listed here is not available to a post.

**Capture and outputs**
- Two smartphone photos, one front view and one side view, plus relevant profile inputs.
- Processing typically takes under 45 seconds. (Not "under a minute", not "45 sec".)
- More than 80 body measurements.
- Body-composition estimates: body fat percentage, lean body mass, and fat mass.
- BMI and basal metabolic rate (BMR).
- Smart Scales: a software-based predicted-weight output with an average prediction
  error of approximately 3.5% under evaluated conditions.
- A 3D body model that supports visual comparison between check-ins.

**Accuracy and repeatability** (internal validation; state as internal)
- Approximately 96-97% agreement across the evaluated body metrics, against expert
  pattern-maker manual measurements.
- Typical absolute error generally 1.5 to 2.0 cm, varying by measurement and body part.
- For most evaluated measurements, typical scan-to-scan differences of less than 1 cm.
- Disclosed population: ages 16 to 78, heights 150 to 220 cm, weights 38 to 210 kg,
  participants from the US and Europe. Populations outside that scope require separate
  evaluation.
- Do NOT combine the accuracy benchmark and the repeatability figure into one claim.

**Privacy and compliance**
- HIPAA compliance in US healthcare environments; adheres to GDPR principles for
  European deployments. Data encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Input photos deleted immediately after processing or retained for up to 30 days per
  the business client's instructions; temporarily retained photos are automatically blurred.

**Boundaries (non-negotiable)**
- FitXpress is not a medical device. It does not diagnose conditions, make clinical
  decisions, determine treatment eligibility, prescribe a fitness program, or establish
  the cause of a measured change.
- Body-composition estimates and predicted weight are supporting outputs; not equivalent
  to DXA, professional BIA, or a calibrated scale.
- The coach remains responsible for interpretation and program decisions.
