Your coach lives three time zones away. Your progress still shows up on the same screen.

Remote coaching used to run on shaky inputs: a home scale that swings with hydration, a progress photo shot in whatever light the bathroom had, a tape measure held at a different angle every time. Any of those can move without your body moving at all.

A guided scan changes the input. Two smartphone photos, one front and one side, processed in under 45 seconds into more than 80 body measurements and a 3D model. Same instructions every check-in, so what you and your coach compare is actually comparable.

The side-by-side view does the real work here: your current 3D model next to the last one, captured the same way both times. That is a steady picture of what actually changed.

The scan does not coach you. It gives your coach something reliable to read. 💪
