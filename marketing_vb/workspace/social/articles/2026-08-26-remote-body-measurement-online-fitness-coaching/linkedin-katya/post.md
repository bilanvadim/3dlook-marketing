Most remote measurement tools get bought for outputs nobody uses.

FitXpress can return more than 80 measurements, body composition, BMI, predicted weight, and a 3D model. Buying all of it is easy. Saying what each number decides is harder.

Here's what separates programs that scale from programs that stall. The ones that scale pick a small set of outputs and tie each to a coaching decision before launch. The ones that stall ship everything and wait for the data to find a use.

More outputs mean more work at capture. More work lowers completion. Low completion kills the scan-to-scan comparison you bought the tool for.

FitXpress fits here. A program configures the required outputs and one comparison view, then adds more once the first one earns its place.

My question: on your last rollout, how many of the metrics you shipped actually changed a coaching conversation?
