A verification tool that accepts camera-roll uploads isn't verifying much.

Generative AI made fake body photos cheap. Our CEO asked ChatGPT and Gemini to show her 27 kg heavier than she is. Both took seconds. One kept her real face and widened the body, the kind of image that could sit beside a genuine headshot.

When a buyer evaluates a verification vendor, I'd start with one question. Is photo capture done in-session, or can the patient upload from the camera roll?

If the camera roll is open, the rest of the demo matters less. Liveness, clothing checks, structured records, they all sit on top of that one control.

It's a cheap question to ask early. It can save a pilot that was never going to hold up.

Which control would you refuse to compromise on when choosing a verification vendor?
