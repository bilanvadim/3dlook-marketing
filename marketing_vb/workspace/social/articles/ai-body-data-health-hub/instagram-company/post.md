Two photos from a smartphone. One scan. A structured, repeatable body measurement record that can be compared over time.

Most health programs still run on a different kind of input: a member's stated weight. A number typed into a form. No provenance, no consistency, captured once and rarely repeated.

The gap between those two inputs — a self-reported number and a verified scan — is where programs lose members, waste review time, and struggle to show defensible outcomes to payers and regulators.

FitXpress doesn't make clinical or eligibility decisions. It standardizes how body data enters a workflow, so the people who do make those decisions have better input. 80+ measurements, body composition, and a 3D model — all from two photos in under 45 seconds, with scan-to-scan repeatability under 1 cm.

When progress is visible, members stay. When data is structured, reviewers spend less time chasing inputs and more time making decisions.
