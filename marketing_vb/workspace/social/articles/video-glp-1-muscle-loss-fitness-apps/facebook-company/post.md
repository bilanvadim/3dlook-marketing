In some studies of glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) treatment, lean mass made up 40% to 60% of the weight lost. In others, it was approximately 15% or less.

Both ranges sit in one 2024 review, by Neeland, Linge and Birkenfeld. It adds a second point. Lean mass is not muscle: it also covers organs, bone, fluids and water in fat tissue.

These are findings across study groups. They do not establish what one member's loss was made of. If an app quotes one range to members, it should quote both.

What can the app do? A 2025 joint advisory from four obesity, nutrition and lifestyle medicine groups supports strength training, alongside adequate protein intake. An app that offers workouts can support one part of that guidance.
