The scale said nothing changed. Your body said otherwise.

Eight weeks into a wellness program, the number on the scale can barely move. Meanwhile the waist has come in, while chest and shoulders stay about the same.

That change is real. It just doesn't show up on a scale.

This is where a body scan earns its place. The first scan sets a baseline. A follow-up turns it into a dated comparison you can actually see, with the two 3D models side by side and the measurements that shifted marked clearly.

Weight is still a useful trend. It just can't tell you where the change happened.
