  range, adjusted to the program goal and expected rate of change."
- **Medical framing:** "FitXpress is not a medical device. It does not diagnose
  conditions, make clinical decisions, or determine treatment eligibility." Use
  this sentence directly if medical framing comes up; never "positioned as."
- **Privacy/storage (only if a post touches data handling):** stored in Amazon S3
  with mandatory SSE-S3 encryption; TLS in transit. Photos are permanently
  removed immediately after processing or within 30 days depending on client
  retention requirements; temporarily retained photos are automatically blurred.
  End-user images are not shared with third parties. FitXpress does not receive
  names, contact details, or other direct identifiers connecting a scan to a
  specific person.
- **GDPR (only if a post touches compliance):** article's exact wording is "In
  most enterprise deployments, the customer acts as the controller and 3DLOOK
  acts as the processor under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)."
  Use this phrasing, not a paraphrase.
- **Core thesis (safe to paraphrase, not a number):** scale weight can stay flat
  while waist, hip, or chest measurements move; a second (follow-up) scan turns a
  single baseline into a dated, repeatable comparison. Central evaluation
  criterion: whether the second scan produces a comparison members and program
  teams can actually use.
- **No-go for this article:** nothing implying FitXpress scores, diagnoses,
