## instagram-company

Two photos. One clear explanation. That's the whole ask.

Remote body scanning asks something a little unusual: hold still for a front photo, then a side photo. That needs context, or it feels strange.

What makes it work in a telehealth flow: telling the patient what the two photos are for, how the numbers get used, and how long the images are kept, before the capture step, not buried in a policy page nobody opens.

It also means planning for the patient who cannot or prefers not to scan. A private space, a decent phone, the ability to stand for a moment: not every patient has all three. A remote program needs another route that still counts.

And the output is an estimate, useful for tracking change between visits, not a stand-in for a clinician's judgment.
