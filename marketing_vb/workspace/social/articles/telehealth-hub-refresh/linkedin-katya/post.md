A conversation I keep having with digital health founders and operators across Israel and the Gulf: the product is ready to scale, but the data feeding it is still whatever the patient typed into a form.

That gap does not show up in a demo. It shows up months into a remote program, when a care team tries to compare this month's numbers to last month's and cannot tell if the change is real or just noise from how it was measured.

Buyers in this region move fast and ask sharp questions before they commit. The ones I trust most are asking about the data layer specifically: is body-data capture consistent, is it documented, and does it hold up once volume goes from a pilot to a full population.

Structured mobile capture is one answer. Two smartphone photos, a guided flow, results back in under 45 seconds, the same method every time. It is a support layer for the care team's review, not a replacement for it.

What are you seeing from buyers in this region when they evaluate a remote program's data layer? More on the workflow side in the full piece.
