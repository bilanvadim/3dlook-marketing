Where the data physically lives decides more deals than the demo does.

I've watched a scanning tool pass the product review and still stall. The blocker is a procurement question: where does this data actually sit?

Most vendor pages skip it. FitXpress states it plainly: hosted on Amazon Web Services, primarily in the US-West-2 region and partially in US-East-1. Not a vague "the cloud." A named region you can check.

So the first thing I ask a scanning vendor is simple. Tell me where the data is stored, and put it in writing.

If they won't name the region, I take that as my answer. A written answer beats a confident verbal one every time, and it's what your legal team will ask for anyway.
