You take two photos. Your face is hidden before the result even loads.

Face obfuscation happens at capture, whatever the retention setting. From there, photos are deleted immediately after processing, or kept up to 30 days under a customer's policy. Any photo held that long is automatically blurred.

What stays is body data, never an identity. Scan records carry randomly generated IDs, and 3DLOOK cannot identify a specific person from the stored records.

The full scan returns structured outputs in under 45 seconds. Privacy here starts in the first second of capture.

Everyone asks what a body scan produces. Fewer ask what it keeps and what it forgets on purpose. That second question is worth answering before you trust any tool with a photo.
