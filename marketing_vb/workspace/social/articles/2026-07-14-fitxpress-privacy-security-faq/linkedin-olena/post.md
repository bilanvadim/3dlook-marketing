In a body-scanning deployment, the vendor rarely owns the privacy risk. You do.

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), in most enterprise deployments the customer acts as the data controller and the technology provider acts as the data processor. As controller, you carry obligations the vendor cannot take off your hands.

Three of them stay yours: the lawful basis for processing, the privacy notices to end users, and consent where the workflow needs it. Get any one wrong and it's your organization that answers for it.

The first thing I ask any scanning vendor: will you sign a Data Processing Agreement with Standard Contractual Clauses, and does it name who does what?

FitXpress publishes that answer in writing, with the roles and the hedge left in.
