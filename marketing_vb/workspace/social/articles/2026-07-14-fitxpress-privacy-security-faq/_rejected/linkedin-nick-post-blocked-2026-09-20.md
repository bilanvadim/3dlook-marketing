No software is "HIPAA compliant." That label doesn't exist.

HIPAA is a regulatory framework, not a certification. When a body-data vendor prints "HIPAA compliant" on a slide, treat it as a claim to verify.

For a GLP-1 or telehealth program, the real questions are narrower. Will the vendor act as a business associate under an executed BAA? Do they accept that both sides carry the HIPAA obligations tied to their own roles and systems?

FitXpress can support HIPAA-governed deployments where 3DLOOK acts as a business associate under an executed BAA, where applicable. Encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and audit logging back it. The rest of the responsibility stays with your team.

Here's what I'd ask any vendor before signing: once our patient data flows through your system, who owns which HIPAA obligation?
