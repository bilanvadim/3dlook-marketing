## Claims discipline for this article

Numbers and boundaries a post may state, in the article's live wording only.
Anything not listed here is not available to a post. This is a trust FAQ (Type G):
the wording **is** the product. `brand-assets/product-info/compliance.md` is derived
from this same live page and carries the full say / never-say table. Read it before
writing any compliance line.

**Capture and outputs**
- Two smartphone photos and a short profile.
- 80+ body measurements, body composition estimates, a 3D model, and Body Progress
  comparisons between scans.
- A scan begins with a guided capture flow and returns structured outputs **in under
  45 seconds**. (Not "under a minute", not "45 sec".)
- Enterprise integration is via API or SDK. Named workflows: telehealth, weight
  management, wellness, connected fitness, insurance, research.

**Data lifecycle — the only two numbers in this article**
- Photos: deleted immediately after processing, or retained **up to 30 days** under a
  customer-specific policy, per the customer's instructions. Retained photos are
  automatically blurred; face obfuscation is applied at capture whatever the policy.
- Measurements, body composition and 3D models: **stored by 3DLOOK on an ongoing
  basis**, unless the customer agreement says otherwise or a deletion request applies.
- Deletion is by scan identifier, at the customer's request. 3DLOOK cannot identify a
  specific individual from stored scan records.
- **Never write that FitXpress "processes but does not store" data.** Outputs are stored.
  Do not promise self-serve deletion endpoints or a backup cycle; the FAQ names neither.

**Roles, ownership, identifiers**
- Verbatim, keep the hedge: "In most enterprise deployments, the customer acts as the
  data controller and 3DLOOK acts as the data processor under GDPR."
- Scan records carry anonymized, randomly generated IDs, not user-identifying information.
- 3DLOOK does not use production customer data to train its models. Do not escalate that
  to "we never use any data" — aggregated or anonymized data may be used where the
  agreement permits.
- 3DLOOK does not sell customer data and does not use it for advertising.
- Body Progress compares two scans by scan ID, chosen by the customer. 3DLOOK does not
  track individuals and does not determine whether two scans belong to the same person.
  Never "FitXpress tracks each patient over time".

**Security — state only these**
- TLS in transit. Amazon S3 server-side encryption with S3-managed keys (SSE-S3), on by
  default, cannot be disabled.
- RBAC and least-privilege provisioning, separated development and production, tenant
  isolation, API key authentication.
- Penetration testing conducted regularly by an independent third-party firm.
- Hosted on AWS, primarily US-West-2 and partially US-East-1.
- NOT ours to claim: KMS or customer-managed keys, "annual" pen testing, ISO 27001,
  EU data residency, "region per client", named monitoring vendors.

**Regulatory — four hard fails, each has one approved sentence**
- HIPAA: "FitXpress can support HIPAA-governed deployments where 3DLOOK acts as a
  business associate under an executed BAA, where applicable." NEVER "HIPAA compliant".
- SOC 2: "3DLOOK is working toward obtaining a SOC 2 Attestation Report and has completed
  an initial readiness assessment aligned with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria."
  NEVER "SOC 2 certified", "SOC 2 compliant", "SOC 2 Type II".
- Medical device, short form: **"FitXpress is not a medical device."** Never "MDR does
  not apply", never "medical-device certifications don't apply".
- FDA: "FitXpress is not cleared, authorized, or approved by the FDA", and 3DLOOK makes
  no representation as to whether clearance is required for a particular use case.
  Never "FDA-cleared", never "no FDA clearance is needed".
- Never "no personal identifiers", "zero personal data", "we don't process PHI". Photos,
  measurements and 3D models may be personal data when linkable to an identifiable
  individual; PHI depends on who holds the data and in what context.
- Not biometric by default: outputs are not produced or used for identification or
  authentication, and facial features are not processed for identification.

**Boundaries (non-negotiable)**
- Supports: remote intake and body-measurement capture, body composition outputs and
  Body Progress comparisons, structured data collection for research protocols,
  structured intake for customer-managed eligibility workflows, member engagement.
- Does NOT independently determine: a diagnosis, treatment, medication recommendation,
  fitness for duty, employment eligibility, insurance eligibility, clinical-trial
  eligibility, or any other high-impact individual decision. Final decisions stay with
  the customer's clinicians, underwriters or designated decision-makers.
- Documentation route: the standard procurement channel or legal@3dlook.me. Do not
  promise a document the FAQ does not list.

**The natural angle for this pack.** The article's value is that it answers procurement
and legal questions in public, in writing, with the qualifications attached. A post that
turns it into a compliance boast inverts it. Point at a question the reader has had to
ask on a call, and link.

**How the gate actually reads these strings** (measured on this pack, 2026-09-20 —
two posts were blocked by exactly these two cases).
- The linter is negation-aware but NOT attribution-aware. `No software is "HIPAA
  compliant."` passes. `when a vendor prints "HIPAA compliant" on a slide` is a hard
  fail, quotation marks and all. If the angle is built on the misconception, state it
  once in the negated form and never let the bare string appear again.
- A question in quotation marks does not read as a question to the gate, wherever it
  sits. `"Who actually makes the decision?" is a fair thing to ask` hard-fails on
  `makes the decision`. Write "who signs off", "who decides", "where the call sits".
