In enterprise health, a deal rarely stalls at the demo. It stalls at the security and legal review. 🔍

Procurement, legal and security teams arrive with the same list of questions. Where does the data live? What happens to the photos? Who is the data controller? Is this a medical device? What is the SOC 2 status? Vendors usually answer these one at a time, under an NDA, on a call.

We took a different route with FitXpress. Those questions now have a published data, privacy, security and regulatory FAQ that answers them in writing, with the qualifications attached.

A few of the answers it gives directly:

- Photos are deleted immediately after processing, or retained up to 30 days under a customer-specific policy, and retained photos are automatically blurred.
- In most enterprise deployments, the customer acts as the data controller and 3DLOOK acts as the data processor under GDPR.
- FitXpress is not a medical device.
- 3DLOOK is working toward obtaining a SOC 2 Attestation Report and has completed an initial readiness assessment aligned with SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria.

The value here is practical. A legal team can begin its review before the first call, and a scoped answer holds up better than a vague one.
