Photos and body data don't live on the same clock.

In FitXpress, the two smartphone photos are deleted right after processing, or held up to 30 days under a customer-specific policy. Any retained photo is blurred automatically. But the measurements, body composition and 3D model are stored on an ongoing basis, unless your agreement says otherwise.

That split matters when you build a retention workflow. Assume everything expires with the photos, and your data map is wrong from day one.

Here's the practical part. Deletion happens by scan identifier. You send 3DLOOK the scan IDs you want removed.

Before launch, keep a clean record of which scan ID maps to which person on your side. 3DLOOK can't identify an individual from stored records. That mapping lives in your stack, and only there.

How are you handling scan-ID mapping today?
