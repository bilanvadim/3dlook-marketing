HIPAA is not a certificate a vendor can hand you.

It's a regulatory framework, not a certification. No auditor stamps a scanning tool as compliant, whatever a slide claims.

When you vet a body-scanning vendor for a GLP-1 or telehealth program, a certification logo answers the wrong question. Ask whether they'll sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and stand behind it as a business associate.

That is the line that actually protects you. HIPAA obligations follow the role each party plays. The vendor covers its systems. You cover how the workflow is configured and whether protected health information is involved.

FitXpress can support HIPAA-governed deployments where 3DLOOK acts as a business associate under an executed BAA, where applicable.

We published the rest of these answers in writing, with the qualifications attached.

Before you sign, do you ask for the BAA first, or the security review? I'd like to know where your team draws that line.
