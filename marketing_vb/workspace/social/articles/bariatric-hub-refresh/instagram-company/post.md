Two photos on a phone, before the appointment even starts.

That small change moves a lot for bariatric care teams. Right now, body measurements often arrive only at the consultation, and part of the visit gets spent measuring instead of talking.

With a guided front and side scan on the patient's own smartphone, a structured body-data record can be ready before anyone sits down. Results typically return in under 45 seconds. No specialized hardware. No measurement-only visit.

So what does that free? The consultation itself. The time a person actually needs in the room: medical history, comorbidities, surgical risk, patient education.

FitXpress supplies the inputs, not the decisions. Clinical eligibility stays with the care team, and each payer decides what documentation it accepts. FitXpress is not a medical device.

Fewer minutes on the tape measure. More on the patient. That is the shift worth piloting.
