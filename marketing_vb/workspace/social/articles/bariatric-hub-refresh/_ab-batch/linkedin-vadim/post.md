The scans that decide your rollout are the ones that never complete.

Remote capture works well until it reaches a patient who cannot stand unassisted, or who is following the instructions in a second language. That patient still needs a record. If the workflow has no answer, a coordinator improvises one, and records stop being comparable across sites.

Two things I would settle before a pilot starts.

First, write the fallback down. Who books the in-clinic measurement, and at which point in the retake sequence does it trigger?

Second, measure completion twice. Once for patients who finish unaided, once for those who needed staff or caregiver help. A single blended completion rate hides the cost of the second group.

The rest of the pilot is easier than those two.

What share of your remote captures need a second attempt right now, and can you break that number down by site?
