Deployments stall on one unanswered question: who acts when the system raises a flag.

Here is the mechanic. At intake, a program can compare BMI from self-reported height and weight with BMI from the same height and the predicted weight from the scan. A difference above the configured threshold is routed for human review.

That is where the commercial conversation usually goes quiet. The software flags the discrepancy. The program decides whether the patient needs a retake, an in-clinic measurement, or further review. If no one owns that step, the flag becomes a ticket nobody closes, and the technology takes the blame.

Before signing anything, I would put one question to the program itself: which role reviews a flagged record, and what is that person allowed to do next?

How long does a flagged record sit in your queue before someone opens it?
