A scale shows weight. A scan shows change.

Most fitness apps still rely on weight as the primary progress signal. But weight is a composite number — it combines fat, muscle, bone, water, and waste. Two people at the same weight can have very different compositions. One person can lose fat while gaining muscle, with the scale showing no change.

Mobile body scanning changes what is visible. Two smartphone photos generate 80+ measurements, body composition, and a 3D model in under 45 seconds. Scan-to-scan comparison tracks circumference changes by region — waist, hips, arms, thighs — alongside body-fat trends over time.

For fitness apps, this means a progress tab that shows body change directly rather than just a weight trend. For users, it means seeing progress during weeks when the scale does not move.

FitXpress provides the data layer. It does not prescribe workouts or diagnose. It gives apps and coaches standardized input so users see real, regional change beyond the scale.
