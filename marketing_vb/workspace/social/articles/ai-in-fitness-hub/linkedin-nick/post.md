I have been talking to US digital fitness and coaching platforms about how they track client progress. The pattern is consistent: coaches are scaling caseloads while body data still comes from self-reports and a bathroom scale.

A coach managing 50+ remote clients cannot easily confirm whether a reported measurement reflects the body or how it was measured. Progress relies on what a client types in. That is the state of play for most US coaching platforms.

Mobile body scanning changes the input. Two smartphone photos produce 80+ measurements, body composition, and a 3D model in under 45 seconds — with scan-to-scan repeatability under 1 cm. Coaches review scan data without manual measurement. Programme discussions reference a trend, not a client's recollection.

This matters at scale. The global fitness app market was around $12.1 billion in 2025, growing at 13% CAGR. Retention remains the hard problem — seven in ten users quit within three months. Coaches who show visible, structured progress across body regions have a different engagement tool than those who reference weight alone.
