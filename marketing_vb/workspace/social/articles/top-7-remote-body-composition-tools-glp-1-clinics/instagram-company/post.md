Two people lose the same weight. One keeps muscle, the other doesn't. The scale can't tell them apart.

On a GLP-1 program, the number on the scale is only part of the story. Body composition is what shows whether someone is losing fat or losing muscle. And when members can actually see that progress, they tend to stay with the program.

That's where remote body scanning fits. Two photos from a phone turn into 80+ body measurements in under 45 seconds. Scan to scan, changes hold to within less than a centimetre, so small real progress doesn't get lost in the noise.

It doesn't replace a scale, a DXA appointment, or a clinician. It makes progress visible between check-ins, and that visibility is often what keeps someone going.

We compared 7 categories of remote body composition and progress-tracking tools GLP-1 clinics can actually use, from connected scales to mobile scanning. Full breakdown at the link in bio. 📲
