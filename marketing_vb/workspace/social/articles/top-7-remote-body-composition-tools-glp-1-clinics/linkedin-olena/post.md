European telehealth and weight-loss programs are running into the same wall: how to track body composition remotely without adding friction for patients or compliance risk for the business.

There's no single best remote tool. A recent comparison from our team looked at seven categories — connected scales, professional BIA, DXA access, mobile body scanning, manual clinic measurement, patient photos, and wearables. Each one fits a different decision and a different moment in the program.

For Continental European operators, three factors shape the choice:

- **Patient burden:** DXA and professional BIA need an in-person visit. Connected scales, wearables, and two-photo mobile scanning capture in seconds, at home. Members stay when progress is visible between check-ins.
- **Data structure:** Some tools deliver raw numbers; others provide structured, longitudinal data that drops into clinical records. That difference matters when you're scaling across multiple markets.
- **Privacy by design:** GDPR isn't a checkbox. It belongs in the procurement conversation, not after. Where data is processed, how long it's stored, and who controls it — these questions should be answered before you sign.

Mobile scanning earns a place in the mix because it produces 80+ measurements in under 45 seconds with scan-to-scan repeatability under 1 cm. FitXpress provides supporting body-data estimates for clinician review. It isn't a medical device and doesn't replace DXA or a calibrated scale where a protocol needs them.

The real work is the mix and the integration: how the data lands in your systems, which categories cover baseline versus frequent check-ins, and whether the vendor handles privacy at the infrastructure level.
