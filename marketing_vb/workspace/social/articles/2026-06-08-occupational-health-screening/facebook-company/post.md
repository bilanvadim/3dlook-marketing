Most occupational health screening still starts with paper forms and a measuring tape. For programs screening hundreds or thousands of candidates, those manual steps add up: incomplete records lead to rescreens, inconsistent measurements mean rework, and every delay keeps someone from starting a job or returning to work.

FitXpress by 3DLOOK moves intake before the appointment. Two smartphone photos, taken at home, return 80+ body measurements and BMI data in about 45 seconds, time-stamped and ready for clinician review.

What changes:
- Intake data arrives before the visit, so clinics open with records in hand
- Structured capture reduces the missing data that causes rescreens
- Return-to-work documentation starts with current measurements, not self-reported recall
- One guided workflow works across clinics, sites, and vendors

FitXpress handles intake and documentation. It does not make clearance or fitness-for-duty decisions. Clinical review stays with the licensed provider.

If your program's bottleneck is the intake step rather than the exam, it is worth a closer look.
