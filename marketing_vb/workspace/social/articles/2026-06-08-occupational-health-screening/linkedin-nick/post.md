I've been talking to occupational health providers and workforce screening vendors across the US. One pattern keeps surfacing.

The bottleneck in screening isn't the medical exam. It's the unstructured intake before it. Paper forms. Tape measurements by hand. Rescreens when a field is missing or inconsistent.

At scale, this compounds. The BLS reported 2.5 million nonfatal workplace injuries in 2024. Each return-to-work case needing a separate clinic visit for intake adds drag. OSHA recordkeeping presumes consistent documentation — free-text notes from a waiting room don't deliver that.

What's changing: digital intake that collects structured body data from a smartphone before the appointment. Two photos, under a minute. The clinic opens the visit with time-stamped, standardized records already attached. The clinician still makes the clearance decision. The intake step just doesn't eat the appointment slot.

FitXpress does exactly this. Pre-appointment capture, structured outputs, HIPAA-maintained and BAA-ready. It supports the intake layer under EEOC/ADA post-offer frameworks, before clinician review and never inside it.

If you're running pre-employment screening or return-to-work clearance at volume, happy to share more.
