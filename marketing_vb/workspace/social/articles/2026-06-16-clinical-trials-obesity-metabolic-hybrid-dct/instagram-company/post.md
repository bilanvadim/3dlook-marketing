Long obesity and metabolic trials ask a lot of the people who join them.

Baseline visit. Follow-ups. Check-ins that can stretch on for a year or more. Many of those visits exist for one reason: to get measured. Weight, waist, hip, body composition, tracked over time so trial teams can see real change.

Some of that measuring can now happen without a trip to the clinic. FitXpress turns two smartphone photos into a guided scan, 80+ measurements and body composition estimates in about 45 seconds. Where a trial's protocol allows it, a participant can check in from home instead of driving in just to be measured.

It's a small shift that can mean fewer unnecessary trips for people already giving months of their time to a study, plus structured, time-stamped records for the teams running it.

FitXpress supports the measurement step. It doesn't replace the methods or decisions a trial's protocol calls for.
