## Format decision
**Recommended:** carousel, 3 slides — per the post's own Design tip, not overridden.
**Why:** The post's angle is a participant-journey narrative (burden of check-in visits → a lighter-weight remote option → what that actually captures), which needs sequence to land — a single static image can't carry "before → shift → outcome" as clearly as three slides swiped in order. This also matches `instagram-company`'s tone in `social-profiles-config.md` ("Visual storytelling... Key stat turned into visual story") and its human-outcome content type, while staying inside the article OG brief's compliance avoids (no patient face/body imagery, no DEXA-style imagery).
**Dimensions:** 1080×1080 px (square) for all 3 slides, consistent aspect ratio across the swipe set. Master each slide at 1350×1350 if extra resolution is needed for repurposing; do not switch to 4:5 mid-carousel — aspect ratio must stay identical slide-to-slide.

## Visual concept
**Big idea (1 sentence):** A packed calendar of check-in visits becomes one lighter at-home check-in — same 80+ measurements, no extra trip.
**Mood:** human, calm, workflow-relief — not clinical/diagnostic, not consumer-fitness-app either. Sits between the article's "trial infrastructure" tone and Instagram's more human register.

## Composition
General frame carried across all 3 slides for carousel continuity:
- Full-bleed dark teal-blue background (see Colors below) — same background treatment on every slide so the swipe reads as one object, not three disconnected posts.
- Center-weighted single visual motif per slide (see Carousel breakdown) — one abstract idea per slide, generous negative space, no crowding.
- Bottom-right: 3DLOOK or FitXpress wordmark badge, small, present on every slide (per source article's OG brief instruction that the badge is consistent across content-hub visuals). No logo file currently exists in `brand-assets/` — designer to source current lockup from the Figma website file or confirm with Vadim before placing.
- Slide-position indicator (dots or "1/3" tag) in a top corner, consistent placement across all 3 slides, so the swipe sequence is legible before the viewer taps through.

## Colors
- Background: 3DLOOK Black/near-black `#0A0A0A`–`#000000`, or the dark teal-blue tone referenced in the article's OG brief for the bariatric/insurance-underwriting sibling banners (exact teal HEX not in `colors.md` — designer to sample from Figma blog-banners file per the Note above; do not invent a new teal).
- Primary accent (headline text, icon strokes, slide-indicator dots): 3DLOOK Blue `#2962FF` (marked approximate in `colors.md` — confirm before final export).
- Body/sub text: White `#FFFFFF` at high opacity, or Light Gray `#F5F5F7` for the secondary line.
- Do not introduce Red Alert `#FF3B30` or Green Success `#34C759` — no status/verification connotation belongs in this set.

## Typography
- Headline (per slide): Inter Bold or Black (per `fonts.md`) — large, tight tracking, sized to dominate each slide.
- Sub (per slide): Inter Regular or Medium, ~40-50% the headline size.
- Slide 3 stat ("80+", "~45 seconds"): Inter with tabular nums, set apart as the dominant numeral on that slide.
- Max 2 font weights per slide; no third typeface; keep type scale consistent across all 3 slides (same headline size, same sub size) for carousel cohesion.

## What NOT to do
- No stock-photo "people in office / clinicians shaking hands."
- No generic AI-illustration purple-pink gradients.
- No Font Awesome-style icons stacked on top of text.
- No patient/participant face or realistic body imagery on slide 2 or anywhere else — keep the "checking in at home" idea abstract (silhouette, icon, guided-scan bounding-box motif), not a depicted person.
- No literal map/geography for slide 1's calendar reframe — it's a calendar motif now, not the article's dot-map; don't reintroduce a map.
- No DEXA-style scanning imagery on slide 3's stat card.
- Don't let slide-to-slide backgrounds or type scale drift — the 3 slides must read as one continuous object when swiped.
