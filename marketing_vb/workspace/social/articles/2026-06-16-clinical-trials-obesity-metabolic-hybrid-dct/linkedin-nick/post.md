Every clinical trial buyer conversation I have with US sponsors and CROs eventually lands on the same unglamorous line item: the measurement-only visit.

Obesity and GLP-1 trials run on repeated anthropometric measurement — BMI, waist and hip circumference, body composition — captured at baseline, follow-up, and across a monitoring window that can stretch past twelve months. The protocol reads the same at every site. Execution doesn't. A coordinator at one site measures waist circumference a little differently than a coordinator at another, and that gap compounds across a multi-site study.

The FDA's guidance on digital health technologies for remote data acquisition already gives sponsors a framework to work inside of. What most teams I talk to are still missing is the layer underneath it: a capture workflow that's identical at a site visit or a remote check-in, producing the same structured, time-stamped record either way.

That's the gap FitXpress sits in. Not a DEXA replacement, not an endpoint-validation tool — a standardized way to capture the measurement step across sites, staff, and timepoints, in a workflow US CROs and sponsors running multi-site obesity and GLP-1 programs recognize right away.
