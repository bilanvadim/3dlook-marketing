Obesity and weight-loss drug trials depend on one thing, repeated constantly: consistent body measurements. Weight, waist, hip, body composition, tracked visit after visit so research teams can see real change.

The hard part is consistency. When a trial runs across many sites with different staff doing the measuring, small differences creep in: technique, tools, even the time of day someone is measured. That adds up over a study that can run a year or more.

Our latest article looks at how sponsors and research teams are addressing this: a guided smartphone scan, two photos, no special equipment, producing the same structured measurements every time, at the clinic or as a remote check-in between visits. FitXpress returns 80+ measurements and body composition estimates in about 45 seconds, with every scan time-stamped for the record.

It doesn't replace the reference methods a protocol requires. It keeps the routine measuring consistent across sites, staff, and time.
