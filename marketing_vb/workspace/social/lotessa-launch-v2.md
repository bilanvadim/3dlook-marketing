LINKEDIN

Lotessa, a UK digital wellness and self-management platform for adults managing obesity treatment with GLP-1 medication, is now live on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Congratulations to Tsitsi Chinyandura and the whole Lotessa team on the launch. Lotessa brings medication and side-effect tracking, progress timelines, non-scale victories, educational content, and a moderated community together in one place, giving people a fuller, steadier way to support themselves through treatment.

FitXpress by 3DLOOK is integrated into Lotessa as a supporting feature. Through a smartphone 3D body scan, users can track changes in body measurements and shape over time, alongside everything else Lotessa already tracks. Weight alone rarely tells the whole story during a GLP-1 journey, and measurement and shape changes can offer a useful additional view of progress.

We're glad to support Lotessa as a partner in building a more complete picture of what progress looks like.
