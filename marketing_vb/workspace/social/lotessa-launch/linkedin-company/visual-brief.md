## Format decision
**Recommended:** single static image — partnership announcement card with dual branding.

**Why:** LinkedIn-company audience (telehealth operators, GLP-1 providers, digital health buyers) responds to clean, credible partnership signals. A dual-logo announcement card with a short value statement communicates "Lotessa is live + FitXpress is integrated" in one glance. No carousel needed — this is a launch announcement, not a workflow argument.

**Dimensions:** 1200×627 px (LinkedIn link/feed image). Export a 1600×900 master for repurposing.

---

## Visual concept
**Big idea (1 sentence):** Two brands, one shared belief — progress on a GLP-1 journey is more than the number on the scale.

**Mood:** warm, professional, human — celebratory without being promotional. "A partner we believe in," not "a product we're selling."

---

## Composition
- Full-bleed dark background: 3DLOOK near-black `#0A0A0A` or dark teal-blue from the FitXpress blog banners (designer to sample exact HEX from Figma). Flat or very subtle brand gradient — no purple-pink AI gradient.
- **Top third — Lotessa logo (dominant):** Lotessa logo centred or left-aligned, sized as the primary visual anchor. This is Lotessa's launch moment; their logo leads. If the Lotessa logo includes the wordmark, use the full lockup. If it's an icon-only mark, add "Lotessa" in Inter below it.
- **Centre — value statement:** One short line, set in Inter Bold or SemiBold, white `#FFFFFF` at high opacity:
  > "Medication tracking, community, education, and now 3D body scanning — in one place."
- **Lower third — dual-brand lockup:**
  - Left: "FitXpress by 3DLOOK" wordmark or badge, small
  - Centre: a thin vertical divider (hairline, low-opacity white `#FFFFFF` at ~30%)
  - Right: Lotessa wordmark or badge, small
  - Below the lockup, tiny sub-line: "Now available on the App Store and Google Play" in Inter Regular, Light Gray `#F5F5F7`
- **Bottom-right corner:** 3DLOOK wordmark badge, small, consistent with other content-hub visuals.
- Generous negative space around all elements — this is a launch card, not an infographic. Let the two brand marks breathe.

## What NOT to do
- No stock-photo "people using phones" or "clinicians smiling"
- No generic AI-illustration purple-pink gradients
- No Font Awesome-style icons
- No DEXA/scale/medical-device imagery — this is about a wellness platform, not diagnostic equipment
- No showing the Lotessa app UI as a screenshot — the visual is brand-level, not product-demo
- No "Available now on App Store and Google Play" badge graphics (iOS/Android store badges) — keep these out of the visual; the caption carries the links
- Don't make FitXpress the visual hero — it's a supporting feature, not the announcement subject
- Don't use red/green success/fail colour coding
