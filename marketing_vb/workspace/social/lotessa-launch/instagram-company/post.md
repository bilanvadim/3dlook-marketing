Big news from our partner Lotessa 🎉

Lotessa, the UK wellness platform for adults managing obesity treatment with GLP-1 medication, is now live on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

In one place: medication tracking, progress timelines, non-scale victories, education, and a supportive community.

Through FitXpress by 3DLOOK, Lotessa users can also take a 3D body scan with just their smartphone, tracking measurements and body shape changes over time. Because progress isn't only what the scales show.

Congratulations to the whole Lotessa team on the launch 👏
