## Format decision
**Recommended:** single square image — Lotessa logo hero card with a warm supporting statement.

**Why:** Instagram-company content is visual storytelling, human angle, less corporate. The post already tells readers the Lotessa logo accompanies it — so make the logo the visual hero. A single clean card with the Lotessa logo + one warm line about progress beyond the scales fits Instagram's feed-scroll rhythm and doesn't compete with the caption's conversational tone. Matches `instagram-company`'s config: "Visual storytelling. Human angle — technology through the lens of real outcomes."

**Dimensions:** 1080×1080 px (square). Export a 1350×1350 master for repurposing.

---

## Visual concept
**Big idea (1 sentence):** Lotessa's logo, centre stage, on a warm dark background — "because progress isn't only what the scales show."

**Mood:** warm, human, celebratory — the tone of supporting a partner's big moment. More personal and less corporate than the LinkedIn card.

---

## Composition
- Full-bleed dark background: 3DLOOK near-black `#0A0A0A` or dark teal-blue from Figma (same palette as LinkedIn card for brand consistency across platforms, but composition and mood differ)
- **Centre — Lotessa logo, hero-sized:** The Lotessa logo sits dead-centre, large, as the dominant visual element. If the logo is a wordmark, size it to ~60% of the 1080×1080 canvas width. If it's an icon+wordmark lockup, let it fill the centre with generous breathing room. This is Lotessa's visual moment.
- **Below the logo, centre-aligned — one warm line:**
  > "Because progress isn't only what the scales show."
  (Inter Medium or Regular, White `#FFFFFF` at ~90% opacity, sized to be legible on mobile — not tiny)
- **Bottom-right corner:** 3DLOOK wordmark badge, small, same treatment as other content-hub visuals. This is supporting branding — don't let it compete with the Lotessa logo.
- Generous negative space — Instagram feed images compete with a lot of visual noise in-feed. Let the Lotessa logo breathe with plenty of dark space around it.

---

## Text on visual
- **Primary:** Lotessa logo (full lockup — icon + wordmark or wordmark-only, as provided by Lotessa team)
- **Supporting line:** "Because progress isn't only what the scales show." (Inter Medium/Regular, White, centre-aligned, below logo)
- **Badge:** 3DLOOK wordmark (bottom-right, small)

Keep text minimal — the Instagram caption carries the full story. The visual exists to make Lotessa's brand recognisable in-feed and deliver the post's core emotional line in one glance.

---

## Colors
- Background: `#0A0A0A` or dark teal-blue (same as LinkedIn — brand consistency, designer to confirm from Figma)
- Text: White `#FFFFFF` at high opacity
- 3DLOOK badge: White wordmark on dark background, no colour accent needed — it's supporting, not leading
- Do not introduce green, red, gold, or any colour that competes with Lotessa's own brand palette
- If Lotessa's logo uses a specific brand colour: let it carry that colour naturally; don't recolour or override it

---

## Typography
- Supporting line: Inter Medium or Regular, sized for mobile legibility (~10-12pt equivalent on a 1080×1080 canvas viewed at phone size)
- Max 1 font weight on the card (Regular or Medium) — the logo does the visual heavy lifting
- No bold, no multiple type sizes

## What NOT to do
- No stock photography — not even "phone in hand" or "person smiling"
- No generic AI purple-pink gradients
- No app-store badge graphics (Apple/Google download buttons)
- No "swipe →" or carousel indicators — this is a single-image post
- No FitXpress phone-capture illustration — this is Lotessa's moment
- No body/face imagery
- No "Available now" text on the visual — the caption says it
- Don't over-design: the Lotessa logo + one warm line + 3DLOOK badge is the entire composition
