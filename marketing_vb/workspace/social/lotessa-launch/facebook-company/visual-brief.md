## Format decision
**Recommended:** single static image — dual-brand card with a short feature list, accessible to both consumer and industry audiences.

**Why:** Facebook-company serves a mixed audience — consumers on GLP-1 journeys plus telehealth/digital health professionals. The post explains what Lotessa offers and how FitXpress fits. A clean, warm dual-brand card with a short feature list mirrors the post's "here's what's in one place" structure. Matches `facebook-company`'s config: "Accessible and community-oriented. Broader audience than LinkedIn — explain without jargon. Slightly warmer."

**Dimensions:** 1200×630 px (Facebook link/feed image). Export a 1600×900 master for repurposing.

---

## Visual concept
**Big idea (1 sentence):** Lotessa brings five things into one place — and now, with FitXpress, users can see their body change over time too.

**Mood:** warm, accessible, human — the tone of a helpful recommendation shared with a community. "Here's something worth knowing about," not "here's our product."

---

## Composition
- Full-bleed dark background: 3DLOOK near-black `#0A0A0A` or dark teal-blue from Figma (brand-consistent with LinkedIn and Instagram cards)
- **Top-left or top-centre — Lotessa logo:** Sized as the primary brand anchor, but not as dominant as the Instagram hero card. This card carries more information; the logo leads the composition without filling it.
- **Below the logo — headline (warm, human):**
  > "Progress on a GLP-1 journey is more than a number on the scale."
  (Inter Bold or SemiBold, White `#FFFFFF`, left-aligned or centre-aligned depending on logo placement)
- **Below the headline — feature row (compact, icon-free):**
  Five short labels, arranged in one or two rows, separated by thin dots or hairline dividers:
  > Medication tracking · Side effects · Progress charts ·
  > Non-scale victories · Education · Community · 3D body scanning
  (Inter Regular, White `#FFFFFF` at ~80-90% opacity, smaller than headline)
  Each label = one short phrase. No icons — text-only, clean, scannable.
  "3D body scanning" is the last label, subtly highlighted in 3DLOOK Blue `#2962FF` (the only colour accent on the card) — it's the "and now this too" reveal.
- **Bottom — dual-brand foot:**
  - Left: "FitXpress by 3DLOOK" wordmark, small
  - Centre: thin vertical divider
  - Right: Lotessa wordmark, small
- **Bottom-right corner:** 3DLOOK wordmark badge, tiny, consistent with other content-hub visuals.
- Generous margins — this is a Facebook feed image, which renders smaller than LinkedIn. Don't crowd the edges.

## Colors
- Background: `#0A0A0A` or dark teal-blue (brand-consistent, designer to confirm from Figma)
- Headline: White `#FFFFFF` at high opacity
- Feature labels: White `#FFFFFF` at ~80-90% opacity
- Accent highlight ("3D body scanning"): 3DLOOK Blue `#2962FF` (marked approximate — confirm from Figma)
- Foot/divider: Light Gray `#F5F5F7` or White at ~30% opacity
- No green, red, gold, or any medical/pass-fail colouring

---

## Typography
- Headline: Inter Bold or SemiBold, large enough for Facebook feed legibility
- Feature labels: Inter Regular, smaller, with comfortable letter-spacing for readability
- Foot: Inter Regular, smallest
- Max 2 font weights; no third typeface
- All text left-aligned or centre-aligned — pick one alignment and keep it consistent across the card

## What NOT to do
- No stock photography — especially not "person on phone" or "person on scale"
- No generic AI purple-pink gradients
- No icons next to each feature label — text-only keeps it clean and scannable
- No app-store badge graphics (Apple/Google download buttons)
- No comparison "before/after" layout — this is a launch announcement, not a product demo
- No body/face imagery
- Don't make it look like a product page or app landing screen — it's a social post visual for a broad Facebook audience
