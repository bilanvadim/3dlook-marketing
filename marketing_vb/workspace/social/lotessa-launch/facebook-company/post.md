Lotessa, a UK digital wellness and self-management platform for adults managing obesity treatment with GLP-1 medication, is now available on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Lotessa brings together medication and side-effect tracking, progress timelines, non-scale victories, educational content, and a moderated community, giving people one place to manage the different parts of their treatment journey.

FitXpress by 3DLOOK is integrated into the platform as a supporting feature. Through a quick smartphone 3D body scan, Lotessa users can track changes in body measurements and shape over time, a helpful complement to weight alone, which doesn't always capture the full picture of progress.

Congratulations to Tsitsi Chinyandura and the Lotessa team on the launch, and welcome to everyone downloading the app.
