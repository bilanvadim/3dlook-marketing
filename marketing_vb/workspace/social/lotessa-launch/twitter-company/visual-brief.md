## Format decision
**Recommended:** single image — partnership stat card, dual-brand with one punchy claim.

**Why:** Twitter/X rewards a single sharp, legible claim over dense composition. The main post is 222 characters — one clean announcement. The visual should mirror that: two brand marks + one line that captures the core message. Matches `twitter-company`'s config: "Punchy, data-first. One sharp insight — не переказ."

**Dimensions:** 1200×675 px (16:9, standard X/Twitter in-feed image). Export a 1600×900 master for repurposing.

---

## Visual concept
**Big idea (1 sentence):** Lotessa × FitXpress — "track body measurements and shape, not just weight" — in one glance.

**Mood:** crisp, confident, data-informed — the visual equivalent of a sharp tweet. No warmth overkill (Twitter audience skews industry), but not cold either.

---

## Composition
- Full-bleed dark background: 3DLOOK near-black `#0A0A0A` or dark teal-blue from Figma (brand-consistent across all platforms)
- **Top area — Lotessa logo:** Left-aligned or centre-top, medium-sized — not hero-scale like Instagram, not feature-list scale like Facebook. Clean brand presence.
- **Centre — the claim (dominant, punchy):**
  > "Track body measurements and shape, not just weight."
  (Inter Bold or SemiBold, White `#FFFFFF`, large, centre-weighted or left-aligned — this is the visual's main payload)
- **Below the claim — dual-brand connector, small:**
  > "Lotessa  ×  FitXpress by 3DLOOK"
  (Inter Regular, Light Gray `#F5F5F7` or Mid Gray `#6B7280`, small, centred or aligned with the claim)
  The "×" (multiplication sign, not letter x) acts as the partnership connector — no "powered by" or "featuring"
- **Bottom-right corner:** 3DLOOK wordmark badge, tiny, consistent with other content-hub visuals
- Generous negative space — this is a single-claim card for fast-scrolling Twitter. One line, two names, done.

## Text on visual
- **Primary claim:** "Track body measurements and shape, not just weight." (Inter Bold/SemiBold, White, large — this is 90% of the card's visual weight)
- **Connector:** "Lotessa  ×  FitXpress by 3DLOOK" (Inter Regular, Light Gray, small)
- **Badge:** 3DLOOK wordmark (bottom-right, tiny)
- No "Now available on App Store and Google Play" — the follow-up reply tweet carries the links
- No hashtags on the visual — Twitter hashtags belong in the tweet body

---

## Colors
- Background: `#0A0A0A` or dark teal-blue (brand-consistent, designer to confirm from Figma)
- Primary claim: White `#FFFFFF` at full opacity
- Connector: Light Gray `#F5F5F7` or Mid Gray `#6B7280`
- 3DLOOK badge: White wordmark, no colour accent
- No blue accent on this card — the claim is the focus; let it stay monochrome (white + gray on dark). If a subtle accent is desired, colour the "×" connector in 3DLOOK Blue `#2962FF` — but only if it improves legibility, not for decoration.

---

## Typography
- Claim: Inter Bold or SemiBold, large — sized to be the dominant element on a 1200×675 canvas
- Connector: Inter Regular, ~25-30% of the claim's font size
- Badge: Inter (any weight), tiny
- Max 2 font weights; no third typeface
- Consistent alignment across the card (centre-aligned recommended — Twitter images render in a tall feed column, centre-weighting reads well)

## What NOT to do
- No stock photography — not even abstract
- No generic AI purple-pink gradients
- No app-store badge graphics
- No "download" arrows or CTA buttons — the follow-up reply tweet has the links
- No body/face imagery
- No icon next to the claim — text-only
- Don't overcrowd: this is a 1200×675 canvas carrying one sentence + two brand names. Negative space is the design.
