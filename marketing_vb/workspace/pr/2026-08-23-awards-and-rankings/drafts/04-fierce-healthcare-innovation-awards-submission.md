## Submission narrative

**The problem:** Weight, BMI and body composition are used across healthcare, in telehealth intake,
GLP-1 and weight-loss program eligibility, online-pharmacy prescribing, insurance underwriting and
employer wellness programs, but the data usually comes from self-report. Self-reported weight and
measurements are inconsistent and, in some workflows, subject to deliberate mismatch between what a
patient reports and their actual measurements.

**The innovation, FitXpress:** FitXpress replaces self-report with a 45-second, two-photo smartphone
scan that returns BMI, body composition (fat %, lean mass, fat mass, essential fat, beneficial fat)
and 80+ measurements at 96-97% accuracy against manual measurement. It's delivered as an API/SDK that
health companies embed directly into their own patient- or member-facing apps; 3DLOOK doesn't operate
a consumer-facing product itself.

**Where it's already in production:**
- **UK Meds**, an online pharmacy, uses FitXpress for BMI verification as part of its prescribing
  workflow.
- **Yazen**, a weight-loss program, built FitXpress into longitudinal member tracking and ran 34,000
  scans through it in 2025.
- **Healthyr** uses it to complement patient profiles.

**Why this belongs in a healthcare innovation program specifically:** FitXpress is built to operate
inside regulated health workflows: data handling aligned with the Health Insurance Portability and
Accountability Act (HIPAA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), photos deleted
immediately or within 30 days per customer policy, and no processing of personal identifiers beyond
what the scan itself requires. The measurement layer is designed to be audit-ready, because the
customers using it (pharmacies, telehealth providers, insurers) answer to regulators who will ask
where a number came from.

**Scale to date:** 112,100 scans across 67 active customers in 2025, with FitXpress contributing to
$822K of 3DLOOK's $1.084M in annual recurring revenue that comes from enterprise-integrated accounts.
