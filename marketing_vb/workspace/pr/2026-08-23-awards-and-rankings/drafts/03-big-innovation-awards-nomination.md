## Nomination narrative

**The innovation:** A patented statistical generative human body model that reconstructs a full 3D
body and 80+ measurements from two ordinary smartphone photos, in under 45 seconds, at 96-97% accuracy
against manual measurement (error margin 1.5-2.0 cm) and 95%+ repeatability across repeat scans of the
same person (variance under 1 cm). The model is trained on 9+ years of proprietary data: 150,000+
photos, 30,000+ 3D scans and 430,000+ individual measurements.

**Why it's innovative rather than incremental:** Most body-measurement tools either need dedicated
hardware (3D scanners, depth cameras) or trade accuracy for convenience. 3DLOOK's model runs on a
standard phone camera and still hits accuracy tight enough for health-compliance decisions, whether
that's a telehealth provider verifying eligibility, an insurer verifying underwriting data, or a
pharmacy verifying a prescription-relevant BMI. That combination of phone-only capture,
clinical-adjacent accuracy and a 45-second turnaround is the hard part to replicate, more than any
single measurement taken in isolation.

**Real-world validation:** 67 active customers ran 112,100 scans through the platform in 2025. UK
Meds uses it for BMI verification inside an online-pharmacy prescribing workflow. Yazen built it into
a weight-loss program that ran 34,000 scans in 2025. Safariland has used it in custom-fit PPE
manufacturing for more than five years. Data is processed under HIPAA-aligned and GDPR-aligned
handling, with photos deleted immediately or within 30 days depending on customer policy.

**Business impact:** $822K of 3DLOOK's $1.084M 2025 annual recurring revenue comes from enterprise
contracts, deep integrations rather than one-off usage, across 67 active customers, out of a base of
100+ total customers served since founding in 2016.
