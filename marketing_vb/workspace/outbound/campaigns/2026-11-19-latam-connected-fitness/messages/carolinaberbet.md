For a coach routine, a test could look like this. One group of members on a coach plan scans at the start and at the first check-in, and a comparable group follows the routine as usual, assigned randomly where practical.

We count scan completion, retakes, and day-30 and day-90 retention. Criteria are set before the start, and the scan opens to more members only if they are met. Coaches' decisions and members who skip the scan stay as they are.
