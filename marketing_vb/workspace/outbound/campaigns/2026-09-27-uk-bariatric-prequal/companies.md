## Coverage gaps / risks

1. **The pool is genuinely small, and the hypothesis predicted this correctly.** Flavour 1/2
   (specialist bariatric groups) produced exactly **7** qualifying companies against a
   working estimate of "8-15" — at the low end, and only after excluding 9 real UK bariatric
   businesses purely on headcount. **The UK private bariatric-surgery market outside the
   large hospital groups is structurally a solo-consultant-practice market.** Every
   independent clinic found in this research beyond the 7 listed is a single named surgeon
   plus a small support team (dietitian, nurse, coordinator) — real businesses, doing real
   volume, but not clearing a 10-person floor. This is not a search-depth problem: roughly
   20 independent searches surfaced the same small set of multi-surgeon, multi-site brands
   repeatedly (Healthier Weight, Tonic, The Hospital Group, Streamline, NOSC, The Bariatric
   Group, Phoenix Health) and a long tail of solo practices that never grows past ~5 pages
   of results.
2. **True flavour-2 (surgery + endoscopic + GLP-1 under one intake, run by a company above
   the headcount floor) was not found as a standalone company.** The closest matches
   (Weight Doctors UK Ltd, GV Weight Loss Clinic, Dr Andrew Jenkinson) are all excluded on
   size. In practice, flavour 2 shows up as a *feature* inside flavour-1 accounts already on
   this list — Healthier Weight (ESG), The Hospital Group (GLP-1-vs-surgery content), Tonic
   (balloon) — rather than as its own segment of companies. Message-sequencer should treat
   flavour 2 as an angle within these accounts, not expect separate flavour-2 target
   companies.
3. **No bariatric reference customer exists**, as the hypothesis already accepts. Proof in
   copy stays limited to the anonymised "34K scans" line per Vadim's decision 4.
4. **Employee and revenue figures are desk estimates**, not Sales Navigator confirmations —
   several are explicitly ranged (e.g. "~50-200") because no public filing or reliable
   third-party number was found in this pass. Step 3's Sales Navigator company-size filter
   will sharpen these; none of the estimates here are load-bearing for the validate-companies
   gate (which passed on fit and geo, not on precise headcount).
5. **Registered-office evidence corrected three HQ-city guesses mid-build** (Ramsay: London,
   not Basingstoke; Practice Plus Group: Bristol, not Reading; The Hospital Group: Rochdale/
   Greater Manchester, not Birmingham) — a reminder that `evidence_geo` keyword hits are weak
   and `evidence_registered_office` is the stronger signal, exactly as the runner brief warns.
6. **Nuffield Health cross-profile question is still open** (Detailed notes #9) — needs a
   quick check with whoever owns the occupational-health ICP conversation before Katerina
   sends, not before this list is built.
7. **This list does not reach the 20-company target, and should not be padded to get there.**
   15 clears the stated floor of 12 honestly. Expansion beyond this scope (pan-European
   flavour-1/2 on `olena`, or widening flavour 2 to non-surgical endoscopic-only providers)
   is Vadim's call per hypothesis.md's own "Expansion options," not something to backfill
   into this campaign's list.
