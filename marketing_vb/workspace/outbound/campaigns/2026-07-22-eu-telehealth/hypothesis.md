# Hypothesis: EU Telehealth & Digital Health Platforms

## Use case
European telehealth platforms, digital health companies, and virtual care providers need structured body data to improve patient engagement, track treatment progress remotely, and verify body composition for weight-management and clinical programs.

## 3 evidence-backed reasons this segment matters now

1. **EU digital health regulation is maturing.** DiGA (Germany), NHS Digital, and EU Health Data Space are creating frameworks that reward measurable outcomes. Platforms need objective body data — not self-reported weight — to demonstrate program efficacy.

2. **GLP-1 adoption in Europe is accelerating.** Novo Nordisk (Denmark) is expanding Wegovy availability, creating downstream demand for remote body composition tracking in weight-management programs. Platforms like Oviva, Yazen, and Wellster are already building for this market.

3. **Terveystalo (Finland's largest healthcare provider, €1.3B revenue)** has 170 contacts in this list — from clinical directors to tech leads. They're expanding digital health services and evaluating body measurement tools for their telehealth stack.

## Target buyer personas

**P1 — Direct clinical buyers:**
- Chief Medical Officer / Medical Director / Head of Clinical Operations
- Head of Virtual Care / Head of Digital Health / Head of Telemedicine
- Clinical Director — Weight Management / Obesity / Metabolic Health

**P2 — Adjacent decision influencers:**
- Head of Product / Head of Digital Transformation (at digital health platforms)
- Director of Member Experience / Patient Engagement
- Head of Growth / Revenue Operations (at subscription-based health platforms)

**P3 — Executive sponsors + Technical entry points:**
- CEO / COO / CFO / CMO (at digital health companies)
- CTO / CIO (at health-tech platforms — PASS P3, can open doors or delegate to team)

**WEAK — Secondary:**
- IT/technical roles (Technical Director, Solutions Architect, Tech Lead) — `technical-integration` angle
- Manager-level roles in clinical/health departments (close to topic, no budget)
- Board members / Advisory Board (strategic, not operational)

## Anti-cases

- Pure hospitals/clinics with no digital/virtual care program (orthopedic surgeon at Terveystalo ≠ digital health buyer)
- Non-health-tech IT companies (telecom, fintech — even if CDO title)
- Dental/Optical-only divisions
- Biotech/pharma with no patient-facing digital program
- Companies based outside Europe (Bahrain, Kosovo — unless EU-registered entity)
