UK-based (England-HQ) private GLP-1 / weight-loss telehealth providers running subscription longitudinal-care programs (direct-to-consumer, cash-pay, ongoing coaching + prescribing). Not NHS trusts, not the large multiline online pharmacies, not US platforms.

## Sub-segment

## Use case (1 sentence)
They can drop a 2-photo FitXpress scan into their app to verify BMI at eligibility AND track body composition + lean-mass longitudinally across repeat check-ins — cutting month 2-3 drop-off and giving them a defensible, audit-ready verification trail as UK online weight-loss prescribing rules tighten.

## Target buyer persona
- **Title:** Head of Clinical Operations (primary). Secondary: Chief Medical Officer / Medical Director; Head of Member Engagement / Retention.
- **Why this title:** Clinical Operations owns both eligibility-verification (regulatory exposure) and check-in cadence (retention) — the two points this use case touches. CMO co-signs the clinical/lean-mass angle. Growth/Product leaders care about retention but not compliance, so they're a weaker single entry point given the regulatory trigger.
- **What they care about (KPIs):** 6-month adherence/retention, month 2-3 drop-off, cost per qualified prescription, compliance audit pass rate/defensibility, GLP-1 program ROI, lean-mass preservation as clinical differentiator.
- **Likely objections:** "We already collect a photo / use a smart scale" → verification vs. self-report fraud, Smart Scales mismatch detection, repeatability. "Is this GDPR/clinically safe?" → GDPR, photo deletion, no personal identifiers, not positioned as diagnostic. "More onboarding friction?" → 45-second, 2-photo, in-app. "Will regulators accept app-based BMI?" → positioned as operational verification supporting the clinician's decision, not the clinical judgment itself.
