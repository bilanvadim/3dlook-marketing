# Target Companies — 2026-07-08-uk-telehealth

> Sub-segment (from `hypothesis.md`): England-headquartered, private (cash-pay, D2C) GLP-1 / weight-loss telehealth providers running subscription longitudinal-care programs with repeat 30/60/90-day+ check-ins and in-house or partnered clinical prescribing. ~$2M+ revenue, roughly 50-500 employees. Excludes NHS trusts/pathways, large multiline online pharmacies where weight-loss is a side SKU (Boots/Pharmacy2U/Superdrug-type), one-time-qualification-only providers, sub-$2M/pre-revenue clinics, existing customers, US-only platforms.

**WebSearch status: worked throughout this research (no blocking).** All companies below were checked against live web sources; URLs are cited per company. Where a field could not be confirmed (employee count, revenue, HQ precision), it is marked "unverified" rather than estimated.

## Summary

- **Shortlist delivered: 17 companies** (requested: 20). See "Coverage gaps / risks" below for why the count landed at 17, not 20 — this is a real finding about how narrow the sub-segment is once anti-cases and the England-HQ/mid-market filters are applied strictly, not a research shortfall.
- **Hypothesis fit: medium.** The top 9 (High + Medium confidence) are strong, well-evidenced fits. The bottom 8 (Low / Low-Medium confidence) are real companies but carry flagged weaknesses (unverified size, boutique scale, geo, or business-model mismatch) — recommend Vadim/message-sequencer treat these as a secondary tier, not equal-priority with the top 9.
- Of the hypothesis's named starting set of 12, **5 kept as strong fits, 1 kept-with-heavy-flag, 1 merged into another entry, 5 dropped** (see "Named-set disposition" below).

## Coverage gaps / risks

- **We did not reach 20.** After applying the England-HQ requirement, the mid-market 50-500 employee / $2M+ revenue band, the "subscription + repeat check-in" requirement, and the anti-cases (NHS-commissioned, multiline pharmacy, one-time-qualification-only, sub-$2M, recently-acquired, non-UK-HQ) strictly, the verifiable universe of good-to-moderate fits is **17**, split roughly 9 well-evidenced (High/Medium) and 8 weaker (Low/Low-Medium, each flagged with the specific reason). This tracks with the general company-researcher heuristic that a shortlist under ~15 signals an overly narrow hypothesis — at 17 we're just above that line, and the shape of the gap is informative: the England-HQ private GLP-1 telehealth space is dominated by a small number of well-known platforms (Voy, Numan, Second Nature, SheMed, CheqUp) plus a long tail of either (a) boutique Harley-Street-style private GP clinics that are too small, or (b) large general online pharmacies where weight loss is one SKU among many (the explicit anti-case) — there isn't a deep "middle" of 50-500-employee, weight-loss-only subscription telehealth companies beyond what's listed here.
- **Two of the strongest-looking names by revenue (Numan) may already have scaled past the "50-500 employees" ceiling** the hypothesis specifies — worth deciding with Vadim whether to keep pursuing at the company level or narrow to a specific buying team (Clinical Ops) regardless of total headcount.
- **Employee/revenue data is frequently unverified or source-conflicting** for UK private health startups (LinkedIn vs. PitchBook vs. Companies House filings often disagree by 2-5x). Every row with a size flag should be re-checked at the people-extractor stage via LinkedIn Sales Navigator headcount filters before final targeting.
- **Oviva UK is the highest-risk inclusion** — its dominant, best-evidenced model is an NHS Tier 3 referral pathway (the explicit anti-case), with only a passing mention of a private channel. Recommend Vadim explicitly confirm (or drop) before this account enters message-sequencer.
- **"Simple.Life" named in the hypothesis does not clearly correspond to a real UK GLP-1 company** — the closest match (Simple Online Pharmacy / Simple Online Healthcare Ltd) is Scotland-HQ, not England. Flagging this as a possible transcription/confusion in the original hypothesis-generation step.
