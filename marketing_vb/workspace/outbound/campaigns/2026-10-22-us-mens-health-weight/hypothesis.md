## Use case

Target, not copy: a men's health business with a weight-loss line embeds a guided two-photo capture (white-label API or web and mobile SDKs) in a patient-facing tool it already runs (portal, patient app or pre-visit flow); patients capture at the start and from home between labs, check-ins and visits; each capture returns 80+ body measurements (waist among them), body composition estimates with BMI and BMR calculated, and a 3D model, and the clinician or coach picks which captures to compare.

### Gameday deployment path (context for lanes and easy outs; never claimed in copy)

FitXpress ships only as an API and SDKs: someone must place the capture step in a web or mobile flow they control; the optional Admin Panel monitors and exports results and does not replace that integration (`tech-spec.md`, `how-it-works.md`). An independently owned Gameday clinic has no such flow. The likely path is HQ: Gameday adds the step once to the patient-facing tools the network runs for every clinic, and each clinic switches it on (under white-label the host places the entry point). Not verified: which patient tools HQ owns or whether a vendor runs them; we claim no vendor integration. A Gameday deal starts at HQ (`network`; Timothy R., `technical-integration`); clinic people are local voices, asked about their own clinic, with a Message 2 easy out to whoever at HQ owns the network's patient tools.

## Why this is plausible (evidence)

1. **The men's health channel now sells weight loss.** Gameday: body composition scans and GLP-1 at select clinics; Maximus: name-brand GLP-1 added 2026-06-11; Hone: a weight-loss formulary, and [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/hone-health/) describes its GLP-1 move; DudeMeds and LaSara: GLP-1 programs (sources in the table).
2. **They already sell body change beyond the scale**, in a clinic (Gameday scans "go beyond the scale") or as an add-on beside quarterly retests (Hone); Maximus takes progress photos through its portal. A phone capture reaches the patient at home and between visits.
3. **Guidance asks for a body-size measure beyond weight and BMI.** The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology Commission on clinical obesity (Jan 2025) confirms excess adiposity by direct body-fat measurement or an anthropometric criterion such as waist circumference, in addition to BMI ([Lancet](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(24)00316-4/fulltext); applied in [PLOS Medicine](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pmed.1004749)). Context for Vadim, never a claim in copy.
4. **FitXpress, with its limits** (`proof-points.md`): the outputs above in under 45 seconds, white-label API and SDK. Limits: composition values are estimates; no study against in-clinic bioimpedance or the add-on scan; no visceral fat output; validation 38-210 kg; no men's-health reference customer.
