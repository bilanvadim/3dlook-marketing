## Use case (1 sentence)

A UK club operator, the club-software or coaching platform behind a white-label app, or a UK workout app adds a FitXpress scan to the progress journey its own app already runs, at onboarding and at each progress check-in (a training-plan review, an assessment, a coach's check-in), giving members 80+ body measurements and body composition estimates from their own phone between club visits or sessions, and giving the company one timestamped record per scan through the API, under its own brand.
