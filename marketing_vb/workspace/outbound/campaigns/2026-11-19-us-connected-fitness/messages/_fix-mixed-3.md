## Campaign-wide rules for this round (every batch gets the same list)

Vadim chose a fix round after QC 11/20. The gate is stricter since this morning: run `check-messages` (or `split-messages`) and get exit 0.

1. **Message 1 names the product.** FitXpress or 3DLOOK appears in every Message 1. Vary the form across people; not everyone gets "FitXpress adds…".
2. **The product sentence is not a template.** About three in four Message 1s carry the same skeleton, "2 (guided) photos on the member's phone return 80+ … as one (timestamped) record", or the colon-list form "FitXpress adds X: 2 photos …". At one company, no two people share the shape of the product sentence. Build it from the person's own hook: what the result would show on that app's own screen for that person's topic. Do not move everyone to a new shared shape.
3. **No labels in front of a number or a link.** Drop "For scale:", "The scale so far is…", "The page for this use case:" and "FitXpress adds the measured half:". Say it in a plain sentence or leave it out. The gate notes these as soft.
4. **US spelling**: personalized, standardized, white-labeled, organization.
5. **"There is no integration fee."** never directly follows the pilot description, because there it reads as a free pilot. Put it with the SDK or integration point, or drop it.
6. **Data and setup claims.** The capture runs on the member's phone, and results come back to the company's app through the API, linked by the scan ID. Never "sits on <company>'s side", "own end to end" or "everything runs on the member's phone". No account has clubs, so never write "no club visit".
7. **Non-buyers** (referral lane, CX heads, Head Coaches, user research, strategy, subscription marketing) get an easy out to the right owner and no pilot pitch.
8. **The repeatability sentence** appears only in the `product`, `coaching` or `science` lanes, verbatim, with nothing added after it.
9. **Card change:** the `product` lane's last question target now reads "which screens around a fixed capture (onboarding, consent, results) the team would want in its own brand". Never ask what the capture would have to match in the app's design: the capture layer stays fixed.
10. **Before you stop,** re-read every person of each company you touched, side by side. A fix must not create a new shared phrase. Then run the gate.
