## Use case (1 sentence)

Inside its own member app, a virtual cardiometabolic program asks a member for a two-photo scan and receives 80+ body measurements (waist and hip circumference included) with body composition estimates and a 3D model, in under 45 seconds from the photos to structured results, adding a standardized, guided body-size record next to the glucose, blood pressure, lab or body data it already collects, with no extra device to ship.

## Why this is plausible (evidence)

1. **Clinical guidance now asks for a body-fat or body-size measure beyond weight and BMI.** The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology Commission on clinical obesity (January 2025) says excess adiposity should be confirmed either by direct measurement of body fat, where available, or by at least one anthropometric criterion (waist circumference, waist-to-hip ratio or waist-to-height ratio) in addition to BMI; at least two anthropometric criteria can confirm it without a high BMI ([Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(24)00316-4/fulltext), criteria as summarised in [PLOS Medicine](https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pmed.1004749)). Circumferences are one of the accepted routes and the one a phone capture serves; FitXpress body composition estimates are never presented as the direct body-fat route. The IAS and ICCR Working Group on Visceral Obesity calls waist circumference a vital sign in clinical practice ([Nature Reviews Endocrinology](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41574-019-0310-7)). Superpower's own guide calls waist-to-hip ratio a low-cost proxy for central adiposity that predicts cardiovascular events ([Superpower](https://superpower.com/blog/body-composition-biomarkers)).
2. **These exact accounts are adding body data beyond the scale right now.** 9amHealth has shown members' waist circumference trends in its app's Data Hub since late 2024 ([App Store release note 3.5.1](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/9amhealth-whole-body-care/id6443864010)) and added a body-composition scale to employer obesity programs in March 2026; the announcement says clinicians "increasingly focus on body composition rather than weight alone" ([HIT Consultant](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/03/04/withings-9amhealth-partnership-remote-patient-monitoring-obesity-care/)). Signos, a CGM-first program, put a phone-camera body composition scan inside its app in July 2026 ([Signos help center](https://support.signos.com/hc/en-us/articles/51683046833428-Body-Scanning-with-the-Signos-App)). The pattern "glucose program adds an in-app body scan" is proven in this list; no phone-camera scan was found at the other three (site, App Store listing, press; 2026-09-29).
3. **The employer channel pays for outcomes it can see.** 9amHealth's Series B leads with projected medical and pharmacy savings at two Fortune 100 employers ([PR Newswire, May 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/9amhealth-raises-26m-to-expand-into-chronic-conditions-driving-the-majority-of-employer-pharmacy-spend-302769972.html)); Level2 refunds employers when fees exceed savings (UHG 2025 brief); Signos names employer benefits its primary growth channel ([MedCity News, May 2026](https://medcitynews.com/2026/05/signos-continuous-glucose-monitor-diabetes-dexcom/)); Superpower started selling to employers through a benefits partner on 2026-09-22 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/superpower-partners-with-sequoia-to-bring-proactive-preventive-health-to-employers-302884980.html)). The Business Group on Health 2026 survey of self-funded employers lists "validating clinical eligibility with objective biometric data" among GLP-1 guardrails ([BGH](https://www.businessgrouphealth.org/newsroom/news-and-press-releases/press-releases/2026-glp-1-survey), [MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2026/05/glp1s-employers-coverage/)). Context for Nick, never numbers in copy.
4. **FitXpress offers circumferences, with stated limits.** From `proof-points.md`: 2 photos (front and side), under 45 seconds from the photos to structured results, 80+ body measurements, body composition outputs (BMI, BMR, fat %, lean mass, fat mass), typical scan-to-scan differences under 1 cm for most evaluated measurements, white-label API and SDK.
