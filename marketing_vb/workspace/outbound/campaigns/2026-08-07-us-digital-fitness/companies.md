## Coverage gaps / risks

- **Ownership-structure noise is the dominant data-quality risk on this list.** At least 4 of the 27 companies (MyFitnessPal, Lose It!, Aaptiv, Personify Health/Virgin Pulse) have gone through a PE acquisition, media-conglomerate acquisition, or merger in the last 3-6 years. This doesn't disqualify them under the "recently acquired" anti-case (none of these are *recent* — all are 2+ years old, so the ICP-shifting/stalled-sales-cycle risk has likely settled), but it does mean the buyer conversation for these four is very unlikely to be a scrappy founder/CPO — expect a larger product org and possibly a longer sales cycle. Flag for message-sequencer to adjust tone/angle accordingly.
- **Revenue-figure reliability is weak across the board for private companies.** Free data aggregators (Growjo, Tracxn, ZoomInfo, PitchBook free tier) frequently disagree by 2-10x on the same company (e.g., FitOn: $124-134M raised vs. $100K-5M estimated revenue; V Shred: $5.9M vs. $100-250M). Every such conflict is flagged "source-conflict" in the table rather than silently resolved — Sales Navigator / direct company disclosure will be needed to size these accurately before prioritizing outreach sequencing.
- **The $1M+ ARR floor is genuinely uncertain for 5 companies** (Caliber, obé Fitness, Jefit, Shotsy, and arguably FitOn given its ad-supported model) — these sit at Low/Low-Medium confidence specifically because the floor can't be confirmed, not because the vertical fit is wrong.
