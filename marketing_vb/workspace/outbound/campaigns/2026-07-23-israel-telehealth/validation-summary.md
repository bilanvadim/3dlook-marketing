## 4. Recommendations

### Priority 1: HMO Digital Health Leaders (Tier 1, Ranks 1-2, 9-10)

**Why:** The four Israeli HMOs (Clalit, Maccabi, Leumit, Meuhedet) collectively cover ~100% of the population. They run obesity, diabetes, and chronic disease programs at population scale. FitXpress as a standardized body-composition layer fits their need for consistent outcome measurement across thousands of patients.

**Target sequence:**
1. **Clalit first** — largest HMO (~4.7M members). Ran Balicer (innovation) + Keren Marom (digital health) + Avi Attia (IT/digital). Oren Beckerman (TytoCare ops inside Clalit) is the ideal warm introduction — he already works at the intersection of telehealth and HMO operations.
2. **Maccabi second** — most digitally mature. Michal Tzuchman (innovation) + Tal Gruenwald (CDO) + Noa Kedem (product). Maccabi's existing digital infrastructure (Maccabi Connect app) makes integration faster.
3. **Meuhedet third** — smaller, faster pilot cycles. Yossi Zigmon (CMO/digital) + Lea Cohen (CAIO). Opportunity to be their marquee digital differentiator.
4. **Leumit** — smallest HMO but has strong digital leadership in Rakefet Jacoby (CIO) and Ran Zahor (CTO).

**Messaging angle:** Population-level body composition standardization for chronic disease programs. Frame as workflow/outcome layer, not measurement API. Reference UK Meds and Yazen as telehealth analogs.

### Priority 2: Telehealth & Digital Therapeutics (Tier 1, Ranks 3-8)

**Why:** These companies are natural product-integration partners. TytoCare already bundles hardware for remote exams; adding body scanning extends the visit. Sweetch and K Health have existing user bases where body composition tracking adds value.

**Target sequence:**
1. **TytoCare** — Elad Lachmanovich (CPO/CTO) and Tamir Gotfried (CBSO). Partnership/bundling play. FitXpress as add-on to TytoCare's remote exam kit.
2. **Sweetch** — Miri Mishor (CCO). Digital therapeutics for metabolic health is directly adjacent. FitXpress provides the objective body data Sweetch's AI needs.
3. **K Health** — Ran Ilan (CAIO) and Yaron Savoray (CFO). AI-driven primary care with weight-related flows. International market exposure (US + Israel).

**Messaging angle:** Complement existing platform with objective body data. Frame as "the body-composition layer that makes your remote care data complete."

### Priority 3: Sheba ARC Innovation Ecosystem (Tier 2, Rank 16)

**Why:** Sheba's ARC is the premier digital health innovation hub in Israel. A pilot through ARC provides validation, clinical evidence, and a pathway to broader health system adoption — not just in Israel but globally through Sheba's international partnerships.

**Targets:** Eyal Zimlichman (Chief Transformation), Avner Halperin (CEO Sheba Impact), Ayelet Geva (Director of Innovation at ARC).

**Messaging angle:** Research collaboration/pilot. Offer the 200-request free trial for a structured obesity/bariatric pilot with ARC's clinical teams. Position as generating Israeli clinical validation data for global health system adoption.

### Priority 4: RPM Platforms (Tier 2, Ranks 11-15)

**Why:** Datos Health and SHL Telemedicine already aggregate remote monitoring data from multiple devices. FitXpress as a new data source in their ecosystem is a low-friction integration play.

**Targets:** Omri Hayet (COO, Datos), Yossi Graff (VP BD, SHL).
