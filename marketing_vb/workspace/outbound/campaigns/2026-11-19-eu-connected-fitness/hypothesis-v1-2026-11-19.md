## Use case (1 sentence)

A Continental European club operator or fitness platform adds a FitXpress scan to its own member app at onboarding and at each check-in, giving members 80+ body measurements and body composition estimates from their own phone between club visits, and giving head office one timestamped record per scan through the API, under its own brand.

## Target buyer persona

**Who buys:** the head-office owner of the member app and its check-in journey. At club operators that is the CPO, Chief Digital Officer, Head or Director of Product, Head of Digital or Head of App. Next to them sit the Product Owners or senior product managers who own the app's progress and onboarding features, and the Head of Member Experience where that role owns the journey. At platforms it is the founder or CEO, CPO or Head of Partnerships.

**Who routes (PPL-1, PPL-4):** the head-office training lead (Head of Training, Head of Fitness, Fitness Director, Head of Personal Training, Head of Coaching). They decide what a trainer reviews with a member, and the page's coach line ("the coach reviews the same record before setting the next program block") is their lane. Every IN account gets at least one person below C-level when the export has one.

- **Why these titles, not others:** the product decision is "a new step in our app", which the member-app owner makes. Training leads are the practitioners, the function that produced most positive replies in `learnings.md` (PPL-4). Partnerships and BD replied most often of any function (PPL-3) and carry group B.
- **What they care about:** app engagement and repeat check-ins; onboarding completion; membership retention (Basic-Fit tracks average length of stay); upgrades to a premium tier; what a trainer has to work with between sessions; no hardware rollout across clubs; data ownership in their own brand.
- **Likely objections:**
  - "We already have a device in the clubs." Answer: it stays; the phone scan covers the check-ins between visits, in the company's app (page).
  - "A scan step will cut onboarding completion." Answer: it can; the product team chooses where it sits, and the pilot compares onboarding completion with the usual flow (page FAQ).
  - "We could build it." Answer: a pre-trained model through SDK and API, with no computer-vision team to hire.
  - "Where is the data hosted?" Answer from `compliance.md` §10 on reply, never in cold copy.
  - "Is this medical?" Answer: "FitXpress is not a medical device."
- **CTO / Head of Engineering / Head of Mobile:** `technical-integration`, at most two per company, never the only person at an account (engineering 1/90, PPL-3).
- **C-level at groups over ~500 staff (CEO, CFO, COO):** `referral`, never P1 for title alone (PPL-1).
- **Not the buyer:** marketing, brand, content, social and PR; CRM, retention and lifecycle managers (0/44 by function, PPL-3; HYP-6); user acquisition; membership sales; club managers, franchise unit owners and individual trainers or instructors; operations and facilities; finance below the CFO; HR; legal and privacy counsel; customer service; engineers below lead; investors and board members.
