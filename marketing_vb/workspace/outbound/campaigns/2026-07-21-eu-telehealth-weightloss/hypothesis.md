# Outbound Hypothesis — 2026-07-21-eu-telehealth-weightloss

## Vertical
European private telehealth and digital health platforms with weight management, obesity, or GLP-1 care programs — plus large private healthcare providers with growing digital/virtual care arms. Not public health systems (NHS equivalents), not US platforms, not pharmacy chains without weight-loss programs.

## Sub-segment
Two overlapping sub-segments:
1. **Pure-play weight loss / obesity telehealth:** Wellster Healthtech (golighter.de — medicinal weight loss), Oviva (nutrition coaching, weight management), Sidekick Health (digital therapeutics, obesity/chronic conditions), Liva Healthcare (obesity, type-2 diabetes digital therapeutics), Nederlandse Obesitas Kliniek (obesity clinics), The Body Clinic. Revenue $2M-50M+, 20-500 employees, cash-pay or insurance-reimbursed subscription programs with repeat check-ins.
2. **General telehealth/digital health platforms with weight management adjacency:** Doktor.se, Min Doktor (Sweden), Qare (France), Dr.Dropin (Nordics), hellocare.ai, Medgate (Switzerland), Doctor.One (Germany), Doktr (Belgium). Revenue $5M+, 50-500+ employees. These platforms already do virtual consultations and could add body scanning as a feature for weight management, preventive health, or GLP-1 programs.

Plus one outlier: **Terveystalo** (Finland's largest private healthcare provider, publicly listed, €1B+ revenue, 16,800+ staff, 370 clinics, digital services 24/7, weight management/dietitian services in-house). Large enough to be a strategic account even though telehealth/weight loss is a subset of their business.

## Use case (1 sentence)
European telehealth/digital health platforms can embed FitXpress 2-photo body scanning into their apps to give patients objective, trackable body measurements for weight management and GLP-1 programs — replacing self-reported weight with verified BMI, cutting churn with 3D progress visualization, and building a defensible compliance trail as European digital health regulations tighten.

## Why this is plausible (3 reasons grounded in evidence)
1. **European digital health regulation is tightening.** Germany's DiGA (Digital Health Applications) framework requires clinical evidence for reimbursed digital therapeutics — companies like Sidekick Health and Oviva already operate under it. The EU Health Data Space (EHDS) regulation is coming. Verified, audit-ready body measurements align with this trajectory. FitXpress's GDPR posture (photo deletion, no personal identifiers, not diagnostic) is already proven.
2. **GLP-1 weight-loss market is expanding rapidly in Europe.** Wellster's golighter.de explicitly targets medicinal weight loss. Oviva and Sidekick both have obesity/chronic-condition programs. As European payers and regulators scrutinize GLP-1 prescribing (echoing the UK GPhC tightening), verified BMI + body composition tracking becomes a compliance differentiator — not just a nice-to-have.
3. **Product fit is evidenced by existing European customers.** Yazen (European telehealth, 34K scans/year) and UK Meds (UK pharmacy, BMI verification) prove FitXpress works in European-regulated health environments. The integration model (API/SDK, 45-second scan, 2 photos) is light enough for the mid-market European digital health companies that can't build this in-house.

## Target buyer persona
- **Primary:** Chief Medical Officer / Medical Director (owns clinical program design + regulatory exposure)
- **Secondary:** Head of Clinical Operations, Head of Product, Head of Member/Patient Engagement, CEO/Founder (at smaller platforms)
- **Tertiary:** Chief Digital Officer, VP Digital Health, Head of Virtual Care (at large platforms like Terveystalo)
- **Why these titles:** CMO/Medical Director touches both compliance (BMI verification for prescribing) and clinical outcomes (retention, lean-mass monitoring). Product/Engagement leads own the app experience where the scan lives. CEO/Founder at sub-50-person platforms is often the only decision-maker.
- **What they care about (KPIs):** patient retention at 3/6/12 months, program completion rates, cost per qualified patient acquisition, clinical audit pass rate, GLP-1 program ROI, NPS/member satisfaction
- **Likely objections:**
  - "We already collect weight data" → self-report fraud problem, Smart Scales mismatch detection, body composition > weight alone
  - "GDPR / data residency concern" → EU-based processing available, photo deletion policy, no personal identifiers in scan data
  - "More app friction for patients" → 45 seconds, 2 photos, users already take selfies
  - "Integration effort" → light API/SDK, Yazen integrated in weeks
  - "Not our core focus right now" → weight management is already a program — this makes it measurable and defensible

## Anti-cases (where it DOESN'T work)
- Public health systems / NHS equivalents — procurement cycle and pricing don't fit
- Pure in-person clinics with no digital/app component
- Pharmacy chains where weight loss is a side SKU, not a program (different ICP segment)
- Dental/optical-only divisions
- Pre-revenue / sub-$1M startups with no integration capacity
- Existing customers: Yazen, UK Meds, Healthyr
- Companies outside Olena's EU market (UK handled by Katerina, AU by Vadim, US by Nick)
