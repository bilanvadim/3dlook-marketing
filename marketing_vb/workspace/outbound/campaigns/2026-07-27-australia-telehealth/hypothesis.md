## Segment 1: Enterprise Health Insurers (382 contacts)

### Companies
- **Medibank** (198 contacts) — Australia's leading private health insurer with health insurance, telehealth, virtual care, and preventive health programs. Includes Medibank Private, Medibank Health Solutions, and Amplar Health.
- **Bupa Australia** (118 contacts) — Private health insurance, telehealth, primary care, aged care, dental, and preventive health services.
- **HCF Australia** (100 contacts) — Not-for-profit health insurer offering private health insurance, telehealth, preventive health, and wellness programs. Includes HCF Eyecare.

### ICP Fit
**Segment:** Health Plans / Employer Wellness (Rewards & Verification)
**Revenue:** $5M+ annual, large enterprise, population-scale programs
**Why they buy:** Member retention, wellness program verification, preventive health engagement, payer outcome reporting

### Buyer Personas
| Persona | Typical Titles | Pain Point Angle |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| Digital Health Executive | Chief Product Officer, Head of Digital Health, CIO | How to add structured body data to existing telehealth/virtual care workflows |
| Clinical Leader | Chief Medical Officer, Group Medical Director, Medical Director | Objective member progress data for clinical programs, audit-ready records |
| Member Experience Owner | Chief of Member Experience, Head of Customer Engagement | Reduce member churn with visible progress, improve wellness program participation |
| Innovation/Strategy Lead | Chief Strategy Officer, Head of Innovation, Head of AI Strategy | Competitive differentiation in digital health, new member engagement tools |
| Wellness Program Owner | Head of Health & Wellbeing, Head of Preventive Health | Verify wellness progress remotely, standardize intake across programs |
| Operations Director | Director of Operations, Head of Healthcare Programs | Reduce manual intake friction, scale programs with structured data |

### Pain Points
1. **Member retention** — members drop off wellness/weight loss programs without visible progress
2. **Self-reported data inaccuracy** — weight/BMI self-reports unreliable, hard to verify at population scale
3. **Manual intake friction** — clinical team burden from manual measurement, slows program scaling
4. **Fragmented longitudinal data** — self-report + scales + photos don't create clean tracking records
5. **Payer outcome reporting** — enterprise partners increasingly expect measurable, defensible outcomes
6. **Program ROI visibility** — insurers need to demonstrate wellness program impact to employers and members

### Hero Message (Enterprise)
> "Verify body progress remotely across your telehealth and preventive health programs. Structured body data from two photos — intake, documentation, progress tracking."

### Key Differentiator
**Repeatability, not one-time accuracy.** Programs built on repeat scans (wellness check-ins, weight management, chronic disease) need measurement consistency where small real changes aren't lost in noise. FitXpress delivers 95%+ repeatability with <1cm variance across repeat scans.

**Key capabilities for this campaign:**
- Remote intake and progress tracking — no clinic visit required
- 96-97% accuracy vs manual measurements
- 95%+ repeatability (<1cm variance across repeat scans)
- HIPAA-compliant, GDPR-aligned
- API/SDK integration into existing platforms
- Audit-ready records with longitudinal tracking

**Pricing:** $1K/500 req to $10K/20K req. Free trial: 200 req/month.
