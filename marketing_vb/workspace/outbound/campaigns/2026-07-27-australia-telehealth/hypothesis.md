# Outbound Hypothesis — 2026-07-16-au-telehealth

## Vertical
Australian private health insurance + digital telehealth platforms

## Sub-segment
Australia's "Big 3" health insurers (Medibank, Bupa Australia, HCF) and digital-native telehealth/weight-loss platforms (Mosh, InstantScripts). Focus on decision-makers with telehealth, virtual care, preventive health, digital transformation, and member experience mandates. Mid-to-enterprise scale ($500M+ revenue for insurers, $10M+ for digital platforms).

## Use case (1 sentence)
Australian health insurers and telehealth platforms can integrate 3DLOOK FitXpress AI-powered body measurements into their virtual care and preventive health programs — enabling remote progress tracking, objective body data for weight management, and verified BMI for GLP-1 prescribing without in-person visits.

## Why this is plausible (3 reasons grounded in evidence)
1. **Australia's telehealth market is mature and growing** — Australia was an early telehealth adopter with Medicare-subsidised virtual consultations since 2020. Private insurers (Medibank, Bupa, HCF) now compete on digital health experience, telehealth access, and preventive care programs as differentiators.
2. **GLP-1 weight loss is exploding in Australia** — Mosh, InstantScripts, and Medmate are all offering GLP-1 prescribing pathways. Insurers are adding weight management to their preventive health suites. Remote body measurement verification is a compliance + engagement play for every player in this chain.
3. **FitXpress has proven product-market fit in telehealth/GLP-1** — existing customers Yazen (34K scans/year) and UK Meds (BMI verification) demonstrate the model works in comparable markets (UK/Europe). The same value props (compliance, engagement, workflow efficiency) transfer directly to Australian players.

## Target buyer persona
- **Title:** Chief Medical Officer, Head of Virtual Care, Head of Member Experience, Chief Product Officer, Director of Digital Health, Head of Health Insights, Group Medical Director
- **Why this title:** These are the roles that own telehealth strategy, preventive program design, and member digital experience — all of which FitXpress plugs into
- **What they care about (KPIs):** member engagement rates, program completion, clinical compliance, NPS, cost-per-consultation, preventive health ROI
- **What objections they likely have:** "We already have a telehealth platform," "Not a priority right now," "We'd need to integrate with our existing systems," "Data privacy concerns with Australian health data"

## Anti-cases (where it DOESN'T work)
- Pure in-person clinic models with no telehealth/virtual care offering
- Companies with no weight management / preventive health programs
- Dental/optical-only divisions (excluded from messaging unless they own broader health portfolios)
- Sub-scale digital players (<$5M revenue) with no established clinical programs
