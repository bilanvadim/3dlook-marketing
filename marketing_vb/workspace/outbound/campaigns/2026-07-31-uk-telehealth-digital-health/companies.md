# Target Companies — 2026-07-31-uk-telehealth-digital-health

> Sub-segment (from `hypothesis.md`): England-headquartered private/cash-pay D2C providers, ~$2M+ revenue where verifiable, roughly 50-500 employees, running **ongoing (not one-shot) remote programs with repeat body check-ins** across three widened angles: (1) digital health / cardiometabolic & broader longitudinal programs (incl. menopause, women's health-with-body-comp, habit/behaviour-change), (2) connected & digital fitness with a clinical/outcomes lean, (3) weight & BMI verification providers that also run ongoing care. Deliberately differentiated from the 2026-07-08 UK campaign, which was scoped narrowly to GLP-1 weight-loss telehealth clinics — this campaign widens the net beyond pure GLP-1 prescribers and leads on longitudinal engagement + governance rather than eligibility-gating.

**WebSearch status: worked throughout, no blocking.** Every company below was checked against live sources; employee/revenue figures conflict across data providers (PitchBook/Tracxn/ZoomInfo/Owler/Crunchbase) for nearly every private UK health-tech company researched — this is flagged per-row rather than silently resolved. Where a figure could not be verified at all, it's marked "unverified."

## Summary

- **Shortlist delivered: 15 companies** (requested: 30). This is a real finding, not a research shortfall — see "Coverage gaps / risks" below. The 2026-07-08 UK campaign already harvested the deep, well-evidenced core of England-HQ private GLP-1 telehealth clinics (17 companies). Widening the brief to digital health/cardiometabolic, menopause/women's-health, connected fitness, and bariatric aftercare surfaces a real but *shallower* universe: most candidates are either femtech/wellness platforms whose core product is not yet body-composition-native (a genuine fit gap, not a research gap), or small/early-stage teams below the $2M+/50-employee floor.
- **Fit-confidence breakdown:** 2 High, 4 Medium-High, 4 Medium, 5 Low/Low-Medium. No company here is a slam-dunk "already doing exactly this" the way Voy/Numan/SheMed were for the prior campaign's GLP-1 angle — this list is inherently a **build-the-case** shortlist (product/engagement leads, not compliance/eligibility leads), and message-sequencer should treat the top 6 as primary and the rest as secondary-tier exploratory.
- **Segment split:** Menopause/women's-health-with-body-comp (4: Vira Health/Stella, Peppy, Newson Health/Balance, Hertility), Connected/digital fitness (4: Fiit, Flo Health, The Body Coach App, Sweatcoin), Personalized nutrition/weight-management (1: Zoe), Bariatric surgery aftercare (2: Healthier Weight, Tonic), Digital health/RPM/rehab platforms (3: Huma, Thriva, Physitrack/Champion Health), Community weight-loss (1: Slimming World, flagged model mismatch).

## Detailed company notes (top 10)

### 1. Zoe — London, England
- **Website:** https://joinzoe.com
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/joinzoe (unverified — standard-format guess)
- **Model:** D2C subscription health company founded 2017 by Prof. Tim Spector, Jonathan Wolf, and George Hadjigeorgiou. Core product: at-home test kit (gut microbiome stool test, blood-fat test, continuous glucose monitor) + app delivering personalized nutrition scores, meal-photo AI logging, and a dedicated weight-management program/content vertical.
- **Scale signal:** Total funding $133M (16 rounds); most recent £11.7m raise July 2024 (Coefficient Capital) for US expansion. Revenue and employee counts conflict sharply across sources ($17.5M ZoomInfo vs $36.5M elsewhere; 51-200 LinkedIn vs 483 PitchBook) — flagged, not resolved.
- **Why fit:** Zoe already runs an explicit weight-management vertical inside a longitudinal, subscription, repeat-engagement D2C health app — almost exactly the retention/engagement mechanic the hypothesis targets. A 3D progress visualization and body-composition data point would sit naturally alongside its existing biomarker/nutrition-score dashboard. Deloitte UK Technology Fast 50 winner (2024), well-funded, credible clinical research backing (Nature Medicine study on cardiometabolic outcomes).
- **Buyer:** Head of Product, Chief Medical/Scientific Officer, Head of Member Engagement.
- **Flag:** Verify actual current employee count via Sales Navigator before committing to a "50-500 employee" framing — sources disagree by close to 10x.

### 2. Vira Health (brand: Stella) — London, England
- **Website:** https://www.onstella.com
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/virahealth
- **Model:** Founded 2019 by Andrea Berchowitz and Dr. Rebecca Love. Stella is a menopause digital-health platform combining CBT-based techniques, tailored exercise/diet/lifestyle tracking, and menopause coaching. Distribution is explicitly B2B2C — "available exclusively through partners, such as health insurers, employers and other communities," not open self-serve signup.
- **Scale signal:** $16M total raised (£9m / ~$12M Series A, March 2022, per TechCrunch/UK Tech News); 47 employees per Crunchbase. Acquired fellow UK menopause startup Alva.
- **Why fit:** Directly in the hypothesis's "menopause / women's-health-with-body-comp" angle — menopause-related weight/body-composition change is a named clinical pain point, and Stella already does structured lifestyle/exercise tracking. A body-composition data layer would strengthen its outcomes story to the insurers/employers it sells through.
- **Buyer:** Co-founders (Andrea Berchowitz, Dr. Rebecca Love), Head of Product, Head of Clinical/Partnerships.
- **Flag:** B2B2C distribution model means the buying decision may run through Vira Health's own product/clinical team, not through Vira Health's employer/insurer clients — confirm who owns integration decisions.

## Coverage gaps / risks

- **We did not reach 30 — or even the prior campaign's 17.** After excluding the 2026-07-08 campaign's exhaustive GLP-1 telehealth harvest, existing customers, cross-profile overlaps, and anti-cases (NHS-commissioned, non-England-HQ, recently-acquired, sub-floor size), the verifiable universe of *new* England-HQ private D2C companies fitting the widened brief (menopause/women's-health, connected fitness, cardiometabolic/RPM, bariatric aftercare) is **15**, and only 6 of those (Zoe, Vira Health, Peppy, Newson Health, Healthier Weight, Tonic) are well-evidenced Medium-High/High fits. This is a genuine finding about the widened hypothesis, not a research shortfall: the femtech/connected-fitness/digital-health space in the UK is real and well-funded, but most of the companies in it are **not yet body-composition-native** — they track symptoms, hormones, biomarkers, steps, or workouts, not weight/body composition/3D progress. That's a legitimate wedge (product-expansion pitch) but it means fewer "already doing exactly this" fits than the prior GLP-1-specific campaign found.
- **Employee and revenue figures conflict sharply across data providers for nearly every company** (PitchBook vs. Tracxn vs. ZoomInfo vs. Owler vs. Crunchbase routinely disagree by 2-10x for UK private health-tech companies). Every row with a size flag should be re-verified at the people-extractor stage via LinkedIn Sales Navigator headcount filters, and ideally Companies House for revenue, before final targeting or message personalization.
- **Three companies (Huma, Hertility, Physitrack/Champion Health) are explicit "thematic stretch" inclusions** — real England-HQ companies of the right rough scale, but with a model or product mismatch flagged in the table (B2B/B2G platform motion for Huma; fertility/hormone not body-comp for Hertility; MSK/employer-wellbeing not weight for Physitrack). Recommend Vadim/message-sequencer treat these as exploratory, lowest priority, and confirm the wedge angle before drafting messages.
- **Newson Health Group's revenue figure ("$100M-1B" from one data source) is almost certainly wrong** for a 59-employee company and should not be used in any outreach material without independent Companies House verification.
- **Two companies (Slimming World, Sweatcoin) have flagged model mismatches** (in-person community groups; ad/rewards-driven consumer app) that make them weak fits for a body-scanning API pitch despite meeting the England-HQ/scale bar — kept for completeness per instructions, but recommend deprioritizing unless Vadim wants a genuinely exploratory long-tail.
- **No new online-pharmacy/BMI-verification-only candidates were found** beyond what the 2026-07-08 campaign already exhausted — this angle of the widened hypothesis (segment 3: "online pharmacy / digital-prescriber programs that ALSO run ongoing care") did not surface any *new* England-HQ companies not already covered; the widened net mostly pulled in adjacent digital-health/fitness/menopause companies instead, which is a useful signal for Vadim on where the real whitespace is (or isn't).
