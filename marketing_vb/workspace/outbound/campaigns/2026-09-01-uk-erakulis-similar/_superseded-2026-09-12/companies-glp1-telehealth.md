## Sub-Segment: England-HQ, app-first, cash-pay GLP-1/weight-loss telehealth with branded mobile/web app

### Criteria
- **HQ Location**: England, UK
- **App-First**: Branded mobile app (iOS/Android) or web-first with mobile experience

- **GLP-1 Focus**: Offers Mounjaro (tirzepatide) and/or Wegovy (semaglutide)
