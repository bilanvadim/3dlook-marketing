## Coverage analysis

- **30 companies delivered.** The continental European market for pure-play digital weight loss clinics is significantly smaller than the UK market (where we found 17). To reach 30, we expanded criteria to include telehealth platforms with dedicated weight loss/GLP-1 verticals (Tier 2) and adjacent digital health companies (Tier 3).
- **Tier 1 (HIGH fit, #1-6, 7, 9-11, 16, 21):** 11 companies. Pure digital obesity/weight loss clinics or telehealth platforms with dedicated structured GLP-1 programs. Strongest match for FitXpress ICP segment 1. All verified with enriched profiles — funding, revenue, employee counts, buyer personas.
- **Tier 2 (MEDIUM fit, #8, 13, 15, 17, 20, 22):** 6 companies. Telehealth platforms with weight loss verticals but either generalist approach, async model, or acquired/merged status. Need verification of program depth (longitudinal? repeat check-ins? subscription?).
- **Tier 3 (LOW fit, #12, 14, 18, 19, 23-30):** 13 companies. Either below ICP threshold, adjacent digital health, or no dedicated weight loss program. Included for completeness.
- **Geo distribution:** Germany (5), Sweden (4), France (5), Denmark (2), Norway (2), Poland (2), Switzerland (2), Spain (1), Italy (1), Finland (1), Belgium (1), Netherlands (1), Austria (1), Portugal (1), Iceland (1).
