## Coverage analysis

- **30 companies delivered.** The continental European market for pure-play digital weight loss clinics is significantly smaller than the UK market (where we found 17). To reach 30, we expanded criteria to include telehealth platforms with dedicated weight loss/GLP-1 verticals (Tier 2) and adjacent digital health companies (Tier 3).
- **Tier 1 (HIGH fit, #1-6):** 6 companies. Pure digital obesity/weight loss clinics. Strongest match for FitXpress ICP segment 1. All verified with active websites.
- **Tier 2 (MEDIUM fit, #7-23):** 17 companies. Telehealth platforms with weight loss verticals. Real companies with active services. Need verification of program depth (longitudinal? repeat check-ins? subscription?).
- **Tier 3 (LOW fit, #24-30):** 7 companies. Adjacent digital health. Included for completeness; weak weight-loss-specific focus.
- **Geo distribution:** Germany (5), Sweden (4), France (5), Denmark (2), Norway (2), Poland (2), Switzerland (2), Spain (1), Italy (1), Finland (1), Belgium (1), Netherlands (1), Austria (1), Portugal (1), Iceland (1).
- **Notable gap:** Italy and Spain have very few pure-play digital weight loss companies; the market is dominated by general telehealth and doctor booking platforms.
