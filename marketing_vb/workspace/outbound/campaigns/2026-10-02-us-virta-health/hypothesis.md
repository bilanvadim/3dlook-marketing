## Use case (1 sentence)

Inside the Virta app, a member on Virta for Weight Loss, Weight Loss+ or Diabetes Reversal takes a guided two-photo scan, and the care team receives 80+ body measurements (waist and hip included), lean mass and fat mass estimates and a 3D model in under 45 seconds from the photos to structured results: a standardized, repeatable body record that sits alongside the readings Virta's starter kit already sends, with nothing extra to ship.

## Why this is plausible (evidence)

1. **Virta's own researchers call for lean-mass assessment in future weight-loss studies.** A Virta Health author and Virta's co-founder wrote, in a piece subtitled "A call for more research", that "Future studies of pharmacological, surgical, and lifestyle-driven WL interventions should also assess LBM, physical function, and frailty" ([Obesity 2025, PubMed 39948767](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39948767/)). It is a research-design recommendation and says nothing about Virta's care practice; FitXpress estimates are never offered as an answer to it. Virta lists the paper on its research page as work on lean mass during weight loss ([research](https://www.virtahealth.com/research)). Separately, the Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology Commission on clinical obesity (January 2025) says excess adiposity should be confirmed by direct body-fat measurement or by at least one anthropometric criterion (waist circumference, waist-to-hip or waist-to-height ratio) in addition to BMI ([Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/landia/article/PIIS2213-8587(24)00316-4/fulltext); as verified for `2026-09-29-us-cardiometabolic`). FitXpress body composition values are estimates and are never presented as the direct body-fat route or as a lean-mass assessment in the research sense.
2. **Virta is paid on outcomes measured across populations, and GLP-1s are now inside its model.** "Virta only gets paid if we are successful in delivering real health improvement", with fees at risk for "A1c improvements, Weight loss with or without GLP-1s, Prescription reductions, 0% GLP-1 utilization trend" ([employers page](https://www.virtahealth.com/employers)). The June 2025 guarantees cover GLP-1 utilization trend, guaranteed weight loss and a claims-based ROI ([Virta press, 2025-06-04](https://www.virtahealth.com/press/virta-health-launches-industry-first-cost-guarantees)). 4. **What FitXpress brings, with its limits.** From `proof-points.md`: 2 photos (front and side); under 45 seconds from the photos to structured results; 80+ body measurements; body composition outputs (BMI, BMR, fat %, lean mass, fat mass); typical scan-to-scan differences under 1 cm for most evaluated measurements; white-label API and SDK.
