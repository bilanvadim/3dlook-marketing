## 4. Proposed New Structure (Hub — 12 sections)

1. **Opening: The buyer problem** — Fitness apps have step counting, heart rate, workout logging. What they lack is structured body data that shows actual body change over time.
2. **Why AI in fitness matters now** — Market growth (Statista data), consumer expectations, retention economics.
3. **Body data as the missing layer in fitness AI** — What structured body data provides that step counters and weight scales don't. The gap between consumer fitness data and body composition visibility.
4. **Progress tracking: scan-to-scan comparison** — Body recomposition visibility, why weight alone misleads, how structured measurements show real change. Link to AI body scanning for fitness page.
5. **Personalization: how body data enables tailored workouts and nutrition** — Inputs that make personalization real (not just survey-based). Body shape, proportions, composition as personalization signals.
6. **Digital coaching: virtual trainers and structured body data** — How coaches (human and AI) use body data. Remote coaching workflows. The difference between coaching with and without body data.
7. **AI fitness app landscape** — Categories (workout, nutrition, coaching, body scanning, wearables). Examples (Whoop, Spurfit, Kemptai, Asensei, Bodygee, FitGenie). NOT just 3DLOOK.
8. **Where FitXpress fits** — Structured body data capture layer for fitness apps. Two photos → 80+ measurements + 3D model + body composition estimates. API/SDK integration. Scan-to-scan comparison. Admin panel.
9. **What FitXpress does NOT do** — No diagnosis, no treatment decisions, not a standalone coach, not a medical device, no FDA approval needed (data privacy framework only).
10. **Implementation considerations for fitness companies** — Integration (SDK/API), UX (guided capture, under 45 seconds), privacy (GDPR-aligned, encryption), user adoption.
11. **Buyer/ICP fit** — Connected fitness platforms, digital coaching apps, body transformation apps, corporate wellness platforms. $1M+ revenue. Buyer personas: Founder/CEO, CPO, Head of Growth.
12. **FAQ section** — 6-8 questions covering capabilities, limitations, data privacy, FitXpress boundaries, comparison to other methods.
13. **CTA** — Soft evaluation: "Learn how FitXpress supports structured body data capture for fitness apps."

## 10. Key Terminology / Positioning Rules (from brand assets)

**✅ Use:**
- supports, helps standardize, provides structured records, intake and documentation layer, workflow standardization tool, progress tracking tool
- "structured body data" (not "objective")
- "standardized measurements" (not "objective measurements")
- "repeatable capture" (not "accurate" alone without qualifier)
- "supports coach review" / "supports trainer assessment"

**❌ Never use:**
- revolutionary, game-changing, transforming everything, AI-powered future, unlocking limitless possibilities, cutting-edge
- objective (→ structured/standardized/repeatable)
- reader/audience/the following sections/below
- this article/this guide
- by hand (→ manually)
- plus as connector (→ and)

**Positioning:**
- "We" ONLY when 3DLOOK makes a statement
- "You" ONLY in conversion/onboarding sections
- "Supports clinician review" → "supports coach review" / "supports trainer assessment" (fitness equivalent)
