# Changelog — Revision 2: Editorial Review 2 corrections

## 1. Scope

Review 2 approved the telehealth section ("How to Verify BMI Remotely in a Telehealth Workflow") as substantially correct and asked that it be shortened and de-duplicated, not rewritten for accuracy. All required accuracy fixes were in the **pharmacy sections** — unsubstantiated opening claims, absolute/adversarial language, three pieces of irrelevant or inapplicable supporting evidence, regulatory overreach in the capability list, an unverifiable anonymous customer proof point, and four FitXpress capability descriptions that overstated the product's position (liveness, clothing detection, Smart Scales, audit records). This changelog maps each of the 19 numbered items in `review2-comments.md` to where it was applied in `draft-v5-revision2.md`.
