### Section 4. What is Real-Time Pose Validation (RTPV)?
- H3 (verbatim): `What is Real-Time Pose Validation (RTPV)?`
- Target: 170 words.
- Direct answer first: Real-Time Pose Validation (RTPV) is the real-time pose and tilt validation built into the FitXpress capture SDK. It checks position and framing during capture and guides the user to adjust before each photo.
- Must-cover:
  - Operates during capture; checks position, framing, and phone tilt against the capture requirements; gives real-time guidance; forms the validation layer of the capture flow.
  - Skeletal tracking runs on-device or live in the browser; face obfuscation is applied automatically (one clause, no privacy detour).
  - Required sentence (verbatim, allowed boundary negation): "RTPV is a capture-quality and positioning control. It does not assess posture as a medical, musculoskeletal, or health condition."
  - Implementation: the customer brands the surrounding experience (onboarding, results display). The photo capture layer where RTPV runs is not customizable, by design, to protect measurement consistency.
- Boundary: never posture analysis, health assessment, motion analysis, or clinical validation. Do not call RTPV a trademark or new launch.
- Claims: FX-CQ-001, FX-CQ-002, FX-CQ-004.

### Section 6. What happens when clothing may affect scan quality?
- H3 (verbatim): `What happens when clothing may affect scan quality?`
- Target: 170 words.
- Direct answer first: the Clothing Detector, a capture-quality feature, classifies clothing fit during capture and prompts the user when attire may affect the scan.

  - How oversized, loose, layered, or body-obscuring garments can hide the outline.
  - Canon behavior: classifies fit per scan as sport, regular, or oversized; flags inappropriate attire and prompts the user; includes the flag in the response payload for the customer's team.
  - The flow may direct the user to change clothing or repeat the capture.
- Do not: measures through any clothing; detects every garment issue; "prevents manipulation"; rejects every unsuitable scan.
