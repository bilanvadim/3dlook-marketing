# Changelog — Revision 1

**From:** `draft-v4-publisher-final.md` (approved final, 2026-08-02)
**To:** `draft-v5-revision1.md` (2026-08-05)

This revision is applied on top of v4. Category structure, the core claim set (proof-points.md FX-001–FX-009), and the internal-link map carry over unchanged except where a review comment specifically required a wording or scope change.

## The 10 critical changes

1. **Title–content mismatch fixed.** Title changed from "Top 7 Remote Body Composition Tools for GLP-1 Clinics" to "7 Body Composition and Progress-Tracking Tools for Remote GLP-1 Clinics" (Review 1's exact recommended title). Meta title and meta description updated to match; "Top 7" ranking language removed from both. Intro now states plainly that three categories (professional BIA, DXA, manual measurement) aren't remote and two (progress photos, most wearables) aren't reliable composition methods, and explains why all seven still belong in the evaluation as complementary tools.
2. **Ranking removed.** All numbered category headers ("1. Smart Scales…", "4. Mobile Body Scanning…") replaced with unnumbered H3 headings under a new "The Seven Categories" section. No scoring order implied anywhere in the piece.
3. **Buyer-evaluation value added.** Every one of the seven categories now has: example products/service models, data captured, where capture occurs, hardware required, integration options, main source of variability, best role in a GLP-1 workflow, and questions to ask the vendor. This is new structured content, not present in v4.
4. **"Clinical usefulness" column replaced.** The comparison table's "Clinical usefulness" column is now "Best-supported role," using Review 1's exact suggested entries for all seven categories. The "Cost" column is relabeled "General cost pattern" with a footnote clarifying these are general, unsourced operational patterns, not vendor pricing, per Review 1's condition for keeping cost classifications at all.
5. **FitXpress repeatability claim corrected.** "Scan-to-scan variance runs < 1 cm with 95%+ consistency, which is what lets a program see small real changes…" replaced everywhere with Review 1's approved wording: "For most evaluated measurements, repeated scans showed typical scan-to-scan differences of less than 1 cm," followed by the required caveat sentence about longitudinal comparison and not detecting very small short-term changes. Applied in all three locations: the Mobile Body Scanning category description, "Where FitXpress Fits in the Tool Mix," and the accuracy FAQ.
6. **SOC 2 removed.** The line "HIPAA, GDPR, and SOC 2 where applicable" is gone (confirmed against `brand-assets/product-info/compliance.md`, which states FitXpress is explicitly NOT SOC 2 certified). Replaced with Review 1's approved sentence, restructured only to expand HIPAA and GDPR at first use per CLAUDE.md's abbreviation rule (see "Judgment calls" below). The legal link anchor text ("legal and privacy overview") already described the page accurately; left unchanged.
7. **Privacy language corrected.** "Photos deleted immediately or within 30 days" and "processes no personal identifiers" replaced everywhere with the approved distinction: "Production photos are deleted after processing. Structured outputs and session data are retained according to the customer's deployment and contractual configuration." The absolute "no personal identifiers" claim is dropped entirely, matching the review's point that a customer system can still associate a session ID with a patient record.
8. **Wearable description corrected.** "Wrist and impedance wearables infer composition passively and continuously…" replaced with the approved sentence about device-specific, on-demand estimates and the category being more established for activity/sleep/recovery data. Comparison table now reads "device-dependent" instead of "continuous" for wearables.
9. **DXA used consistently.** "DEXA" replaced with "dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA)" at first mention and "DXA" thereafter, throughout the Article body. "DXA is the reference method most clinicians trust…" softened to the approved sentence about DXA providing detailed fat/lean/bone-mineral estimates and being commonly used as a reference method. "Highest clinical usefulness," "reference-grade," and "a number it can stand behind clinically" all removed.
10. **Unsupported prescriptions removed.** "The verdict most experienced programs reach…", "a reference method sets the baseline…", and "Most programs combine two…" replaced with the two approved conditional sentences ("A hybrid model may suit programs that require periodic in-person assessment…" / "The appropriate combination depends on the program's clinical protocol, patient population, cadence, and operational requirements"). Used in "How to Build the Right Tool Mix," the first FAQ, and "Next Steps."

- Moved the comparison table up, immediately after "Short Answer," so buyers see it before the ~1,800-word category detail section (previously the table came after the categories).
- New section order: intro → short answer → comparison table → why tracking matters now → seven categories → how to build the right tool mix → buyer checklist → where FitXpress fits in the tool mix → where FitXpress fits / where other methods remain necessary (limitations + compliance) → implementation → FAQs → next steps (CTA).
- "What FitXpress Does NOT Do" renamed to "Where FitXpress Fits, and Where Other Methods Remain Necessary" per Review 1, and cut down to the approved scope sentence plus the compliance paragraph. The unrelated fraud-detection sentence is deleted.
- "How to Choose for Your Clinic" (a 7-item narrative checklist) split into two: a short "How to Build the Right Tool Mix" section (conditional/workflow-dependent guidance) and a new "Buyer Checklist" section using Review 1's exact 10 procurement questions, replacing the old general-purpose list.
- "Implementation and Evaluation Considerations" trimmed; the "drops into the patient record and EHR adjacent systems" line replaced with the approved API/SDK sentence.
