## Structural refinements

1. **Merged "What Better GLP-1 Progress Tracking Looks Like" and "How Progress Tracking Fits Into Program Workflows"** (v5 lines 105–131, two sections) into one section, "What Better GLP-1 Progress Tracking Looks Like" (v6 lines 103–119). The five bolded properties (Consistent baseline, Repeatable remote capture, Longitudinal comparison, Body-composition estimates, Clinician-accessible records) are kept once. The old second section's four workflow beats (documentation consistency, review workflow, longitudinal comparison, compliance/reporting pressure) restated the same four ideas already covered by the five properties; they are now folded into a single closing paragraph that carries forward only the one non-redundant idea (the ecosystem-dependent pattern) and preserves the link to the [GLP-1 Compliance Challenge](https://3dlook.ai/content-hub/glp-1-compliance-challenge/) page, which otherwise would have been dropped entirely.
2. **Shortened "Where FitXpress Fits"** from 412 words (v5 lines 133–144) to 320 words (v6 lines 121–132) — a 22% reduction, within the requested 20–25% range. Cuts: the separate five-sentence accuracy-methodology paragraph is condensed into one sentence that links out to the accuracy framework page rather than restating the internal validation protocol; the GDPR/HIPAA/photo-deletion paragraph is tightened to one sentence per fact; the Yazen figure is removed (see item 17); the AI in Fitness hub link is removed (see item 18). Kept: the two approved claims (repeatability < 1 cm, weight MAE ≈3.5%), the API/SDK delivery model, and the UK Meds example.
3. **Renamed "FAQs, Next Steps, and CTA" → "Frequently Asked Questions"** (v6 line 137). "Next Steps" remains as its own `###` heading directly after the FAQ (v6 line 151), unchanged in content.
4. **Shortened the repeated product-boundary disclaimer.** The boundary claim ("FitXpress is operational, not diagnostic, does not replace clinicians or calibrated methods") appeared three times: the scope note near the top (v5 line 37, kept as-is — it frames the whole hub, not just the product), the "Role and Limitations" section (v5 lines 145–147, kept as-is — it is the one place this claim belongs, right before the FAQ), and the closing disclaimer (v5 line 167, which restated it a third time). The closing disclaimer is shortened to drop the restated boundary claim and keep only the two links: "*For data, privacy, and regulatory details, see the [Data, Privacy, Security & Regulatory FAQ] and 3DLOOK's [legal information].*" (v6 line 155).
